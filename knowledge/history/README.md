# Retake: questions 13-20

13. Describe the nature of Tsarist colonial policy in the peripheries, including Azerbaijan.
14. The impact of Russian colonial rule on Azerbaijani national identity in the north: How did a local educated intelligentsia emerge despite the colonial policies of the Russian Empire?
15. Mirza Fathali Akhundov and the Call for Modernization of the Islamic World.
16. Describe the major events of the first stage of the National Liberation Movement: Formation of national parties and organizations ("Qayrat", "Difai", "Defense" (Mudafie) and Musavat (Equality)).
17. Discuss the issues raised by Azerbaijani intellectuals in the First and Second Dumas. Identify the Azerbaijani deputies who were part of the Muslim fraction.
18. Analyze the socio-political situation in Azerbaijan after the February Revolution (March-October 1917). The spread of national unity ideas among the majority of the Azerbaijani people.
19. The political situation in Azerbaijan after October upheaval (1917). Bolshevik attempts to consolidate power in Baku. Anti-Azerbaijan policy of the Baku Soviet Commune headed by S. Shaumyan. The massacre policy in Baku and other provinces (1918): causes and consequences.
20. Activity of the Muslim members at the Transcaucasian Seym. Their role in the Trabzon and Batum Conferences (1918).
