# IELTS Academic: format, timing and scoring

## The four papers

| Paper | Time | Content | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Listening** | ~30 min (+2 min check on computer; +10 min transfer on paper) | 4 parts, 40 questions | each recording heard once. See [Listening strategies](listening-strategies.md) |
| **Reading** | 60 min | 3 passages, 40 questions | no extra transfer time. See [Reading strategies](reading-strategies.md) |
| **Writing** | 60 min | Task 1 (≥150 words, ~20 min) + Task 2 (≥250 words, ~40 min) | Task 2 is worth twice Task 1. See [Task 1](writing-task1-overview.md) / [Task 2](writing-task2-overview.md) |
| **Speaking** | 11–14 min | Part 1 interview (4–5 min), Part 2 long turn (1 min prep + 1–2 min talk), Part 3 discussion (4–5 min) | often on a different day. See [Speaking](speaking-overview-and-strategies.md) |

Listening, Reading and Writing are taken in one sitting (about 2 h 40 min) with no breaks. The
course's trial exams copied this: *"The exam will run from 17:00 to 20:00 and will last three hours"*
(first trial exam, 14 Aug).

## Computer-delivered exam: the teacher's advice

From the LATEST_EXAMS channel (28 Jun 2026):

> If you're planning to take a computer-delivered exam, please contact **Barattson** and register for a
> **trial exam**. Don't forget to share the date of your trial exam and the result with me, so I can help
> you choose exam day and register for the official exam.

Things to practise for the computer test:
- typing essays with the on-screen **word counter** (the app's writing editor counts words too);
- highlighting and notes in Reading;
- answering Listening questions while the audio plays, with only 2 minutes to check at the end;
- bring headphones and a laptop to trial exams. The teacher repeated this before every mock.

## Scoring

- **Listening & Reading**: 1 mark per question → band via conversion tables. Approximate Academic values:

| Raw /40 | Listening band | Reading band (Academic) |
|---|---|---|
| 39–40 | 9.0 | 9.0 |
| 35–36 | 8.0 | 8.0 |
| 32–34 | 7.5 | 7.5 |
| 30–31 | 7.0 | 7.0 |
| 26–29 | 6.5 | 6.5 |
| 23–25 | 6.0 | 6.0 |
| 18–22 | 5.5 | 5.5 |
| 16–17 | 5.0 | 5.0 |

(Exact cut-offs vary slightly between versions; the app uses the standard tables.)

- **Writing & Speaking**: four criteria each, see [Band descriptors](band-descriptors.md).
- **Overall**: average of the four bands rounded to the nearest half (x.25 → x.5, x.75 → next whole band).
