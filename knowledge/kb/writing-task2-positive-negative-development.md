# Positive / Negative Development Essay

This is a type of opinion essay. The question describes a **trend** or change in society and asks you to judge it.

## How to recognise it

- *Is this a positive or negative development?*
- *Is this a positive or negative trend?*
- *Do you think this is a positive or negative development?*

If the question **also** asks *Why…?* or *What are the reasons…?*, it is a two-part [direct question essay](writing-task2-direct-question.md). Answer the reasons in Body 1 and the positive/negative part in Body 2.

## Paragraph plan (teacher's "Is it positive or negative development/trend?")

| Paragraph | Length | What to write |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Introduction** | 2–3 sentences | **Paraphrase the prompt**: rewrite the trend or situation in the prompt. **Thesis statement**: clearly state whether you believe this is a **completely positive** or a **completely negative** development. |
| **2. Body paragraph 1: the first main reason** | 5–6 sentences | **Topic sentence**: introduce your first reason why the development is positive or negative. **Explanation**: explain why or how this happens. **Example**: a concrete example to back up your point. **Result/consequence**: summarise the ultimate impact of this point. |
| **3. Body paragraph 2: the second main reason** | 5–6 sentences | **Topic sentence**: introduce your second reason (or present the opposing side if you took a balanced view). **Explanation**: explain the point in detail. **Example**: a supporting example. **Result/consequence**: summarise the ultimate impact. |
| **4. Conclusion** | 1–2 sentences | **Restatement**: summarise the main points from your body paragraphs. |

The teacher recommends a **one-sided** answer ("completely positive" or "completely negative") with two reasons, as both models do. A balanced answer is allowed, and then Body 2 presents the other side.

## Sentence frames from the models

| Purpose | Frame |
|---|---|
| Paraphrase | *The growing trend of … represents a major shift in how modern society operates today.* / *The trend of … has become increasingly common in modern society.* |
| Thesis | *I completely agree that this change is an entirely positive development because it … and …* |
| Thesis with concession | *While some traditionalists believe that …, I completely agree that … is a highly positive development. This social shift ensures that …* |
| Body openers | *First, …* / *Second, …* |
| Contrast | *In contrast, …* / *By contrast, …* |
| Example | *For instance, data from the … confirms that …* / *For example, a comprehensive study by the … revealed that …* / *A clear example of this is seen in …, where …* / *A clear example of this can be seen in …, where government data shows that …* |
| Result | *This extra time is directly used for …, which makes …* / *This internal stability creates …* / *This sense of personal autonomy reduces … and increases …* |
| Conclusion | *In conclusion, the transition toward … is a thoroughly beneficial change for …. By … and …, this practice ensures that …* |

For a negative answer, swap in: *an entirely negative development / a highly detrimental trend / a thoroughly harmful change for …*

## Model essay 1: Positive

**Question:** *More and more people are choosing to work from home. Is this a positive or negative development?*

> The growing trend of remote work represents a major shift in how modern society operates today. I completely agree that this change is an entirely positive development because it improves daily time management and enhances employee’s mental health.
>
> First, eliminating the daily journey to an office allows individuals to utilize their time much more efficiently. Traditional office employment forces workers to spend hours traveling every week, which leads to physical exhaustion and unnecessary transportation costs. In contrast, remote employees can easily redirect these lost hours toward healthy habits like preparing nutritious food, exercising daily, or getting sufficient sleep. For instance, data from the National Bureau of Economic Research confirms that remote workers save over one hour per day on average. This extra time is directly used for family care, domestic duties, and personal health, which makes daily life much more manageable and less rushed for the average professional.
>
> Second, the structural flexibility of a home workspace directly supports better mental health for the contemporary workforce. Conventional office spaces are often filled with stressful distractions, loud noises, and strict schedules that increase employee anxiety. Working from home allows individuals to control their immediate environment, create a quiet atmosphere, and take brief breaks to recover their focus without constant observation. This sense of personal autonomy reduces professional burnout and increases overall job satisfaction. A clear example of this is seen in modern technology firms, where employees who choose their own hours report much lower stress levels, fewer sick days, and higher morale than those in rigid office settings.
>
> In conclusion, the transition toward remote employment is a thoroughly beneficial change for the global workforce. By saving valuable time on commuting and providing a less stressful environment, this practice ensures that workers enjoy better health and a superior balance between life and work.

*(300 words)*

**Analysis**

- *Thesis:* "an entirely positive development because it improves daily time management and enhances employee's mental health". This gives two reasons, and they become the two body paragraphs.
- *Body 1:* no commute. The example is the **National Bureau of Economic Research** (over one hour saved per day). The result is that daily life becomes "much more manageable and less rushed".
- *Body 2:* flexibility leads to better mental health. The example is modern technology firms. The result is "lower stress levels, fewer sick days, and higher morale".

## Model essay 2: Positive, with a concession

**Question:** *Many people nowadays tend to marry and have children in their thirties rather than at a younger age. Is this a positive or negative development?*

> The trend of delaying marriage and parenthood until a person reaches their thirties has become increasingly common in modern society. While some traditionalists believe that early family planning is ideal, I completely agree that postponing these major milestones is a highly positive development. This social shift ensures that individuals are much more emotionally mature and possess the necessary financial security to raise a stable family.
>
> First, entering marriage and parenthood in one's thirties guarantees a much higher level of psychological and emotional maturity. Younger individuals in their early twenties are often still exploring their personal identities, career paths, and emotional boundaries, which frequently leads to relationship instability. By contrast, individuals in their thirties have accumulated valuable life experiences that make them more patient, resilient, and capable of handling domestic conflicts logically. For example, a comprehensive study by the Institute for Family Studies revealed that couples who marry after the age of thirty experience significantly lower divorce rates than those who wed younger. This internal stability creates a much more nurturing and predictable environment for raising well-adjusted children.
>
> Second, delaying family life provides crucial financial security that is necessary for sustainable household management and child-rearing. Establishing a stable professional career and achieving true financial independence takes considerable time in today's highly competitive global economy. Individuals in their thirties are usually well-settled in their chosen industries, earn higher baseline salaries, and have accumulated savings to comfortably cover the heavy expenses of modern childcare. A clear example of this can be seen in developed countries like South Korea and Japan, where government data shows that older parents are far better equipped to afford quality early childhood education and premium healthcare, thereby minimizing overall financial stress within the home.
>
> In conclusion, the modern inclination to marry and have children in one's thirties is a thoroughly beneficial change for contemporary society. Waiting allows prospective parents to develop essential personal maturity and secure the robust financial resources needed to build stable homes, ultimately leading to stronger marriages and better-supported children.

*(334 words)*

**Analysis**

- The introduction mentions the other side (*"While some traditionalists believe…"*) before stating a firm positive opinion. This is a good way to show awareness of the opposing view without writing a whole paragraph about it.
- Reasons: emotional maturity (example: the **Institute for Family Studies**), then financial security (example: **South Korea and Japan**).

---

## Notes

- The question asks you to *evaluate*, so **use evaluative adjectives**: *beneficial, positive, advantageous, detrimental, harmful, damaging*.
- End each body paragraph with the **result/consequence** sentence. This step is special to the teacher's plan for this essay type.
- Don't describe the causes of the trend unless the question asks *why*.

## Practice questions (real exams)

- In many countries today, people in cities either live alone or in small family units, rather than in large, extended family groups. Is this a positive or negative trend?
