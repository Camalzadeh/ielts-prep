# Cause / Effect Essay

## How to recognise it

Two questions: **why** something is happening, and **what results** it has.

- *What are the causes of this? What effects can be seen as a result of this?*
- *What are the primary causes of this? What are the main effects of this … on people and society?*
- *Why is this happening? What impact does it have on …?*

If the second question asks how to *solve* or *deal with* it, use the [cause/solution plan](writing-task2-cause-solution.md).

## Paragraph plan (teacher's "THE IELTS TASK 2: CAUSE/EFFECT STRUCTURE")

**1. INTRODUCTION**
- **Sentence 1: Paraphrase the topic.** Rewrite the topic statement in your own words using synonyms.
- **Sentence 2: Thesis statement.** Clearly state the main cause and the main effect you will discuss.

**2. BODY PARAGRAPH 1: THE CAUSE**
- **Sentence 1: Topic sentence (introduce the cause).** State the main reason why the problem is happening.
- **Sentences 2 and 3: Explanation.** Explain how or why this cause leads to the problem. Go deeper into detail.
- **Sentence 4: The result.** Show the direct consequence or outcome of this cause on society.
- **Sentence 5: Real-world example.** Provide a short, specific example (mentioning a country or organization) that proves your point.

**3. BODY PARAGRAPH 2: THE EFFECT**
- **Sentence 1: Topic sentence (introduce the effect).** State the most significant impact.
- **Sentences 2–4: Explanation.** Describe the outcome of this effect on individuals or society.
- **Sentence 5: Real-world example.** A short, matching example (a study, statistic or country) that shows this effect.

**4. CONCLUSION**
- **Sentence 1: Restate summary.** Summarise the main cause and the main effect in one final sentence.

*(On the handout, the Sentence 5 line under THE EFFECT was copied from the solution sheet: "…a country that successfully used this solution and saw a drop in the problem". The teacher's models actually use a study or statistic that **shows the effect**, so that is what the plan above says.)*

## Sentence frames from the models

| Purpose | Frame |
|---|---|
| Paraphrase | *All over the world, … has become much more common.* / *In many countries around the world, the number of … is increasing rapidly.* |
| Thesis | *This trend is mainly caused by … and … . Ultimately, … can lead to … for …* |
| Thesis (one sentence) | *This trend is mainly driven by … that …, and it results in … for individuals as well as … for society.* |
| Cause topic sentence | *The main cause behind the rise in … is …* / *The primary cause of rising … is …* |
| Explanation and result | *Today, many …* / *As a result, when …, …* / *Because society now …, …* / *This lack of … means that …, which directly leads to …* / *Furthermore, …* |
| Cause example | *For example, data from the … shows that …* / *For example, a study by … found that …* |
| Effect topic sentence | *The effects of this trend are mostly negative for …* / *The effects of this widespread … are deeply damaging to both individuals and the wider community.* |
| Effect explanation | *The biggest issue is …* / *Without …, … are more likely to …* / *In addition, …* / *For individuals, … . For society, … . Additionally, …* |
| Effect example | *To illustrate, a recent global study found that …* / *To illustrate, the … reported that …* |
| Conclusion | *In conclusion, … are the main reasons why … are increasing. This … deprives … of …, which creates …* / *In conclusion, … are the main reasons why … is skyrocketing. This problem not only … but also …* |

## Model essay 1

**Question:** *Nowadays more and more children are being brought up in single parent families. What are the causes of this? What effects can be seen as a result of this?*

> All over the world, raising children in single-parent families has become much more common. This trend is mainly caused by changing views on marriage and a rising divorce rate among young couples. Ultimately, growing up in this environment can lead to emotional stress and behavioral problems for children involved.
>
> The main cause behind the rise in single-parent families is how the younger generation views marital commitment. Today, many young adults marry at an early age without fully understanding the heavy responsibilities of family life. As a result, when the first serious arguments happen, partners often choose to separate instead of working together to save their relationship. Because society now accepts divorce more easily, young couples are less likely to fight for their marriage, leaving just one parent to raise a child alone. For example, data from the National Center for Health Statistics shows that most divorces involving children under six happen among couples under the age of 25.
>
> The effects of this trend are mostly negative for a child’s development. The biggest issue is the lack of daily attention, care, and love from both parents during childhood. Without a two-parent support system, children are more likely to face emotional struggles, anxiety, or sadness when they compare their family life to their friends' lives. In addition, losing one parent's guidance can hurt a child's school performance and behavior because teenagers are more likely to fall into bad habits without steady rules from both a mother and a father. To illustrate, a recent global study found that children raised in single-parent homes have a 30% higher chance of facing mental health struggles and school suspension compared to those from two-parent homes.
>
> In conclusion, early marriages and a lack of patience between young couples are the main reasons why single-parent families are increasing. This instability deprives children of a complete upbringing, which creates serious emotional and social challenges for their future.

*(318 words)*

**Analysis**

- *Cause:* young people's view of marital commitment: early marriage, separating after the first arguments, and divorce being socially accepted. The example is the **National Center for Health Statistics**.
- *Effect:* the lack of attention and care, which leads to emotional struggles and worse school performance and behaviour. The example is a global study (30% higher chance).

## Model essay 2

**Question:** *Today more people are overweight than ever before. What are the primary causes of this? What are the main effects of this epidemic on people and society?*

> In many countries around the world, the number of people who are overweight is increasing rapidly. This trend is mainly driven by modern office-based jobs that limit physical movement, and it results in severe health problems for individuals as well as heavy financial burdens for society.
>
> The primary cause of rising obesity rates is the inactive nature of modern work cultures. Today, the demands of the contemporary workplace force millions of people to sit at desks in front of computers for most of the day. This lack of daily movement means that people burn far fewer calories than they consume, which directly leads to steady weight gain over time. Furthermore, long working hours leave individuals with very little free time or energy to exercise after their shifts finish. For example, a study by Harvard University found that the average global workweek has increased by 10 hours over the last 40 years, which matches the exact period where global obesity rates tripled.
>
> The effects of this widespread weight gain are deeply damaging to both individuals and the wider community. For individuals, carrying excess weight is directly linked to dangerous, long-term illnesses like heart disease, strokes, and diabetes, which drastically reduce a person's lifespan. For society, these health issues create a massive crisis for public healthcare systems, which must spend billions treating preventable conditions. Additionally, obesity harms the economy because sick employees take more days off work and are less productive when they are at their jobs. To illustrate, the American Diabetes Association reported that diabetes and related weight complications cost the United States $327 billion in a single year, with $90 billion of that total caused entirely by lost workplace productivity and early deaths.
>
> In conclusion, desk-bound office jobs and a lack of daily exercise are the main reasons why obesity is skyrocketing. This problem not only ruins the physical health of individuals but also places a massive financial strain on medical systems and global economies.

*(325 words)*

**Analysis**

- The question asks about effects on **people and society**, so the effect paragraph uses *For individuals, … For society, …*. That answers both parts precisely.
- Statistics make the examples convincing: **Harvard** (the workweek is 10 hours longer and obesity has tripled), and the **American Diabetes Association** ($327 billion).

## Notes

- Keep causes and effects **separate**: all causes in Body 1, all effects in Body 2.
- The thesis should already name the cause and the effect, so the reader knows the plan.
- Useful cause/effect language: *is mainly caused by, is driven by, stems from, leads to, results in, gives rise to, contributes to, as a result, consequently.*
