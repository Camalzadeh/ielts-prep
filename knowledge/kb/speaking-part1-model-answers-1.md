# Speaking Part 1 Model Answers (1): Work, Home, People and Habits

These are the teacher's Part 1 model answers (from *MODEL ANSWERS AND VOCABULARY (ALL)*), reproduced verbatim, with the key vocabulary she attached to each answer. Questions come from *SPEAKING PART 1 QUESTIONS (ALL)*; topics marked **(May-Dec 2026)** are the ones predicted for the current exam season.

**How the model answers are built** — almost every answer is three sentences:
1. a direct answer to the question (often with a softener such as *To be completely honest*, *Generally speaking*, *Without a doubt*);
2. a reason or a concrete detail;
3. an extra point, example or contrast (*Also*, *Plus*, *However*, *On top of that*).

Practise by answering aloud in your own words (20–30 seconds each) and try to reuse two or three of the listed collocations — do not memorise the answers word for word; examiners notice rehearsed speech.

## 1. Work or Studies

### Do you work or are you a student?

Currently, I am balancing my schedule as a part-time student while holding a steady job at a local company. It is quite a busy routine, but I think it is the best way to gain practical experience while getting my university degree. This lifestyle allows me to learn the theory in class and apply it directly to my daily work.

- **Steady job** — A permanent and dependable job.
- **Gain practical experience** *(collocation)* — To get real-world skills by doing a job.
- **Daily work** — Regular everyday work tasks.

### What work do you do?

I work as a marketing assistant, which means I handle social media and analyze customer trends. My role requires a mix of creative thinking and organization to help our company brand stand out. On a normal day, I work closely with graphic designers to make interesting content for our online audience.

- **Analyze customer trends** — To study what products or styles buyers like.
- **Creative thinking** *(collocation)* — The ability to think of new and original ideas.
- **Work closely** *(collocation)* — To work together with someone with a lot of contact.

### What subjects are you studying?

I am currently studying Business Administration, focusing mostly on digital marketing and business strategy. My classes cover everything from basic accounting to customer behavior. I find the lessons on psychology especially interesting because they explain why people buy certain things.

- **Business strategy** — A plan for achieving success in a business.
- **Customer behavior** *(collocation)* — How and why people buy and use products.
- **Basic accounting** — Simple financial record-keeping.

### Why did you choose that job?

I chose this career path because I have always been interested in how good communication can change public opinion. On top of that, the marketing world changes so fast that there is always something new to learn. It really fits my creative personality and my love for solving problems.

- **Public opinion** *(collocation)* — The general view or belief of the public.
- **Top of that** — In addition to that.
- **Solving problems** *(collocation)* — Finding answers to difficult situations.

### Why did you choose to study that subject?

I picked this major because it teaches useful skills that you can use in almost any industry. I wanted a qualification that would help me secure employment and give me many choices after I graduate. Plus, the fast pace of modern business genuinely excites me.

- **Picked this major** — Chose this main subject at university.
- **Secure employment** *(collocation)* — To successfully get or find a job.
- **Fast pace** — Quick speed of movement or change.

### Do you like your job?

Yes, I really enjoy my job because it gives me the freedom to express my ideas. Every day is different, which keeps me excited and stops my routine from becoming boring. Working with a very supportive team makes the whole experience even better.

- **Express my ideas** — To show or say what I am thinking.
- **Every day is different** — A situation where tasks change daily.
- **Supportive team** *(collocation)* — A group of coworkers who help and encourage you.

### Is there anything you dislike about your job?

If I had to pick one downside, it would be the tight deadlines that sometimes cause a lot of stress. When we have to launch multiple projects at the same time, the workload can become very heavy. However, I have learned to manage my time better to handle the pressure.

- **Tight deadlines** — Short time limits to finish work.
- **Heavy workload** — A large amount of work to do.
- **Handle the pressure** *(collocation)* — To deal successfully with stress or difficulty.

### What do you like about your studies?

What I like most about my course is that we learn by looking at real examples of big companies. Instead of just reading textbooks, we are encouraged to solve actual problems that businesses face. This hands-on style of learning makes my time at university very interesting.

- **Real examples** — True cases or stories.
- **Solve actual problems** *(collocation)* — To find answers to genuine difficulties.
- **Hands-on style** — A practical way of doing things.

### What do you dislike about your studies?

The main thing I dislike is the large amount of boring theoretical homework we have to do. Sometimes, what we learn in class feels a bit old-fashioned and disconnected from the real world. I would definitely prefer more practical workshops instead of writing long essays.

- **Theoretical homework** — Assignments based on ideas rather than practice.
- **Old-fashioned** — Outdated.
- **Real world** *(collocation)* — The actual life and business market outside of school.

## 2. Hometown and Countryside

### Please describe your hometown a little.

My hometown is a lively coastal city in the south of my country, famous for its big port and beautiful beaches. It easily mixes old buildings with modern skyscrapers, creating a very unique look. The atmosphere is usually pretty relaxed, but there is always something fun to do.

- **Coastal city** — A city located next to the ocean.
- **Unique look** *(collocation)* — A distinct or special appearance.
- **Relaxed atmosphere** — A calm and comfortable feeling in a place.

### What is your town well-known for?

The city is famous for its fresh seafood, which brings in food lovers from many different places. We are also well-known for hosting a big cultural festival every year with local music, art, and street food. Visitors always talk about how friendly the local people are.

- **Fresh seafood** — Food from the sea that was caught recently.
- **Cultural festival** *(collocation)* — A celebration focused on the traditions and arts of a place.
- **Local people** — The people who live in that specific area.

### Do you like your home town?

Absolutely, I love my hometown because it is where I grew up and made my best childhood memories. I really like the balance it offers between city convenience and natural beauty. It has a warm feeling of home that always makes me feel comfortable.

- **Childhood memories** *(collocation)* — Things you remember from when you were a child.
- **City convenience** — The ease of living near stores, transport, and services.
- **Natural beauty** *(collocation)* — The beautiful scenery of nature (trees, water, hills).

### Is that a big city or a small place?

I would say it is a medium-sized city that is growing very fast because of new businesses. While it does not feel as crowded as a massive capital city, it still has everything you need like big malls and efficient public transport. It gives you the advantages of city life without the terrible traffic.

- **Medium-sized city** — A city that is not tiny but not huge.
- **Public transport** *(collocation)* — Buses, trains, and subways used by the public.
- **Terrible traffic** — Heavy and slow vehicle movement on roads.

### How long have you been living there?

I have lived in this city for my whole life, which is a bit more than twenty years now. Watching the city change from a quiet town into a busy commercial hub has been very interesting. My family has lived in the same neighborhood for a long time, so my roots are here.

- **Whole life** — Entire life.
- **Commercial hub** *(collocation)* — A center for business, trade, and shopping.
- **Same neighborhood** — The exact same area of a city.

### Do you think you will continue living there for a long time?

Even though I love my city, I plan to move abroad soon for my studies and career. I believe leaving my comfortable routine will help me grow up and learn about the world. However, I am pretty sure I will come back here to settle permanently in the future.

- **Move abroad** — To go to a foreign country.
- **Comfortable routine** — A regular pattern of life that feels safe and easy.
- **Settle permanently** *(collocation)* — To move to a place to live there for the rest of your life.

### Would you like to live in the countryside in the future?

The idea of moving to the countryside sounds nice, but maybe only when I am much older. Right now, I rely too much on the fast pace and job opportunities that only a big city can offer. Later in life, though, the peace and quiet of a village would be a great change.

- **Country life / Countryside** — Rural areas away from cities.
- **Job opportunities** *(collocation)* — Chances to find employment or advance your career.
- **Peace and quiet** *(collocation)* — A calm and silent environment.

### Have you ever lived in the countryside?

No, I have spent my entire life living in the city, so I have never experienced country life firsthand. My time in the countryside has been limited to short weekend trips or summer holidays with my family. Because of this, living in a village feels a bit foreign to me.

- **Entire life** — All of my life.
- **Experienced firsthand** — Directly lived through or saw something yourself.
- **Summer holidays** *(collocation)* — School or work vacations during the summer months.

### Do you ever spend time in the countryside?

Yes, I make an effort to visit countryside villages whenever I need a break from the noisy city life. These short weekend trips are the perfect chance to disconnect from my phone and enjoy nature. I find that spending a few days around green fields helps me get my energy back.

- **Make an effort** — To try hard to do something.
- **Noisy city life** — The loud and hectic environment of an urban area.
- **Enjoy nature** *(collocation)* — To appreciate and spend time in outdoor environments.

### What is the difference between living in the countryside and the city?

The main difference is how fast life moves and the amount of environmental pollution. Cities are full of fast-moving traffic, noise, and modern facilities, while the countryside offers a slow pace and clean air. Also, people in small villages usually know each other much better than people in cities.

- **Environmental pollution** — Damage caused to water, air, or land by harmful things.
- **Modern facilities** *(collocation)* — Up-to-date buildings and services like hospitals and transport.
- **Slow pace** *(collocation)* — A gentle or unhurried speed of life.

### What do people living in the countryside like to do?

From what I have seen, country people love outdoor activities like gardening, farming, and walking in nature. They also spend a lot of time together at local events and neighborhood gatherings. Life there is mostly about enjoying a simple and relaxed lifestyle.

- **Outdoor activities** *(collocation)* — Things you do outside for fun or exercise (like hiking).
- **Neighborhood gatherings** *(collocation)* — Meetings or parties where local neighbors come together.
- **Relaxed lifestyle** — A way of living that is calm and free of stress.

### What do you like to do in the countryside?

Whenever I visit rural areas, my absolute favorite thing to do is go for long walks on nature trails. I also really enjoy buying fresh produce like fruits and vegetables directly from local farmers. Just sitting outside with a good book while listening to the birds is something I love.

- **Nature trails** — Walking paths through forests or fields.
- **Fresh produce** *(collocation)* — Freshly harvested fruits, vegetables, and grains.
- **Local farmers** — People who own or work on farms in that specific area.

## 3. Home and Accommodation

### What colour would you choose to paint the walls of your room?

I would definitely choose a soft, simple color like off-white or a very light blue. These bright shades reflect natural light well, making a small room feel much bigger and brighter. Also, calm colors help me rest and relax after a very long and tiring day.

- **Soft color** — A gentle, non-aggressive color shade.
- **Reflect natural light** *(collocation)* — To bounce sunlight around a room to make it brighter.
- **Tiring day** — A day that makes you feel exhausted.

### What colour would you never use in your home?

I would completely avoid using very dark colors like pitch black or aggressive bright shades. These intense colors absorb too much light, which can make a room feel tiny, dark, and stressful. I truly believe a home should be a peaceful environment, not an annoying or distracting space.

- **Pitch black** — Completely dark black.
- **Intense colors** — Very strong or vivid colors.
- **Peaceful environment** *(collocation)* — A calm and quiet setting.

### Can you describe the place where you live?

I live in a modern apartment located in a very safe and convenient residential neighborhood. The flat has an open design with big windows that let in plenty of sunlight during the day. It is decorated with simple furniture to keep the space clean and organized.

- **Residential neighborhood** *(collocation)* — An area of a town where people live, not where businesses are.
- **Open design** — A layout with fewer walls separating rooms.
- **Plenty of sunlight** *(collocation)* — A large amount of natural light from the sun.

### What kind of housing accommodation do you live in?

I currently live in a multi-story apartment building right in the center of the city. It is a modern block that includes helpful features like 24-hour security and an underground parking lot. This type of high-rise building is very common here because city land is limited.

- **Multi-story** — Having many floors or levels.
- **24-hour security** *(collocation)* — Protection and safety monitoring that never stops.
- **High-rise building** *(collocation)* — A very tall apartment or office tower.

### What do you like about your flat?

The thing I like most about my apartment is its prime location, which makes going to work very easy. Also, the flat has a nice balcony where I can sit and enjoy my morning coffee. The walls are also thick enough to block out noise from the busy city traffic outside.

- **Prime location** *(collocation)* — An excellent, central, or highly valued location.
- **Morning coffee** — The first coffee of the day.
- **Block out noise** *(collocation)* — To stop sounds from entering a room.

### Which room does your family spend most of the time in?

Without a doubt, the living room is the main place where my family gathers every single evening. It has a big, comfortable couch and a television, making it the perfect spot for family bonding. We use this room to talk about our day, watch movies, and just have fun together.

- **Without a doubt** — Certainly; 100% true.
- **Living room** — The main relaxation room in a home.
- **Family bonding** *(collocation)* — The process of family members becoming closer by spending time together.

### What can you see from the windows where you live?

When I look out of my living room window, I can see the busy city skyline and a nice local park. It is a beautiful view that shows modern architecture on one side and green trees on the other. At night, the view gets even better when all the city lights turn on.

- **City skyline** — The outline of buildings against the sky.
- **Modern architecture** *(collocation)* — Contemporary styles and designs of buildings.
- **City lights** — The lights of a city visible at night.

### Do you prefer living in a house or a flat?

Personally, I prefer living in a spacious house rather than a small apartment. A house usually gives you more privacy, a backyard garden, and the freedom to change things without strict rules. However, I do admit that apartments are easier to clean and offer better security.

- **Spacious house** — A home with plenty of room inside.
- **Backyard garden** *(collocation)* — A private green area at the back of a house.
- **Better security** — Higher level of safety from theft or danger.

### What would you like to change in your flat?

If I had the chance, I would love to remodel the kitchen to make it bigger and more open. The current setup feels a bit tight and does not have enough counter space for cooking big meals. I would also love to upgrade to newer, smarter kitchen appliances.

- **Remodel the kitchen** — To renovate or change the design of the kitchen.
- **Counter space** *(collocation)* — The flat working surfaces in a kitchen.
- **Kitchen appliances** — Equipment like fridges, ovens, and blenders.

### What do you dislike about your flat?

The biggest problem with my current apartment is the lack of storage space for extra belongings. Because the flat is a bit small, it takes a lot of effort to keep the rooms tidy and avoid physical clutter. Plus, the monthly building management fees are a bit too expensive.

- **Storage space** *(collocation)* — Room or closets used for keeping extra items.
- **Physical clutter** *(collocation)* — A messy collection of objects taking up space.
- **Management fees** — Money paid for the upkeep of a building.

## 4. Teachers (May-Dec 2026)

### Would you like to be a teacher?

To be completely honest, I don't think I have the patience required to be a teacher. While I respect the profession, managing a classroom full of noisy kids sounds very stressful to me. I prefer working in an office environment where I only have to deal with adults.

- **Patience required** — The amount of calm endurance needed for a task.
- **Managing a classroom** *(collocation)* — Controlling and organizing students in a lessons room.
- **Office environment** — A professional workplace setting.

### Do you think you could be a teacher?

If I really had to do it, I think I could teach well because I am good at explaining complex concepts simply. However, my lack of patience might make it difficult to handle bad behavior in class. Being a good teacher requires a lot of emotional strength that I don't have yet.

- **Complex concepts** *(collocation)* — Difficult or advanced ideas.
- **Bad behavior** — Actions that are disruptive or naughty.
- **Emotional strength** *(collocation)* — Mental resilience to handle stress and feelings.

### Did (Do) you have a favourite teacher?

Yes, back in high school, my English teacher, Mrs. Smith, was someone I really respected. She had a unique approach to making old classic books feel exciting and meaningful to teenagers. Her infectious enthusiasm for the subject completely changed the way I look at reading and writing.

- **Unique approach** *(collocation)* — A special or distinct method of doing something.
- **Classic books** — Famous historical literature.
- **Infectious enthusiasm** *(collocation)* — Excitement that quickly spreads to other people.

### How does this teacher help you? / How has your favourite teacher helped you?

She went beyond her normal duty by teaching me how to think clearly and express my ideas well. Whenever I had trouble writing essays, she would stay after class to provide guidance and fix my mistakes. Her belief in me gave me the confidence to start writing my own stories.

- **Express my ideas** — To communicate thoughts effectively.
- **Provide guidance** *(collocation)* — To give helpful advice or instruction to someone.
- **Confidence** — Trust in your own abilities.

## 5. Social Media (May-Dec 2026)

### Do you think you spend too much time on social media?

Sadly, I have to admit that I often spend too much time scrolling through these apps. I usually open my phone just to check a quick message, but end up wasting an hour without realizing it. It has become a bad habit, so I am trying to set daily screen time limits now.

- **Scrolling through apps** — Moving down a phone screen to read content.
- **Bad habit** *(collocation)* — A negative routine that is difficult to stop.
- **Time limits** — Set boundaries on how long you can do an activity.

### What do people often do on social media?

Mostly, people use social media to share updates about their personal lives using photos and videos. It also acts as a major news source where people find out about world events and pop culture. Additionally, many people use it just for fun to watch funny videos or text friends.

- **Personal lives** *(collocation)* — The private everyday experiences of individuals.
- **News source** *(collocation)* — A place or platform where people get information about recent events.
- **Pop culture** — Modern entertainment, fashion, and trends.

## 6. Dreams and Ambitions (May-Dec 2026)

### What is your dream job?

My ultimate goal is to become a creative director for a large international advertising company. In this dream role, I would lead big design projects and provide mentorship to talented young artists from different countries. It would perfectly combine my love for art with my business skills.

- **Ultimate goal** — Final or most important objective.
- **Provide mentorship** *(collocation)* — To offer guidance and training to younger or less experienced people.
- **Business skills** — Abilities related to commerce and management.

### What was your dream when you were a child?

When I was a little kid, I was amazed by space and wanted to be an astronaut. I used to spend hours reading books about planets and looking through a toy telescope from my window. As I grew up, my interests changed to business, but I still love space.

- **Little kid** — A young child.
- **Toy telescope** *(collocation)* — A small, basic plastic magnifier used by children to look at stars.
- **As I grew up** — Over the course of childhood into adulthood.

### Are you the kind of person who sticks to dreams?

Generally speaking, I am a hardworking person who stays focused on my goals even when situations become difficult. I believe that reaching a big dream requires patience, effort, and sustained focus. However, I am also realistic enough to change my goals if my life changes completely.

- **Hardworking person** — Someone who puts a lot of effort into tasks.
- **Sustained focus** *(collocation)* — Continuous concentration on a goal over a long period.
- **Realistic** — Sensible and practical about what can be achieved.

### Do you think you are an ambitious person? / Are you an ambitious person?

Yes, I would definitely describe myself as an ambitious person who always wants to improve. I am never happy with just staying in one place, so I always set high goals for my career and personal growth. This internal motivation forces me to try new things and take smart risks.

- **Ambitious person** — Someone with a strong desire to succeed.
- **High goals** *(collocation)* — Ambitious targets or milestones.
- **Internal motivation** *(collocation)* — Personal drive or desire to achieve something from within yourself.

## 7. Mirrors (May-Dec 2026)

### Would you use mirrors to decorate your room?

Yes, I really like using mirrors as decorations to make a room look nicer. When you put them opposite a window, they reflect the light and make a small space look much bigger. Plus, a mirror with a pretty frame adds a stylish touch to an empty wall.

- **Decorations** — Items used to make a room look attractive.
- **Reflect the light** *(collocation)* — To bounce light back from a surface.
- **Stylish touch** *(collocation)* — A small elegant or fashionable detail.

### Do you usually take a mirror with you?

No, I rarely carry a pocket mirror with me when I go out. Since modern smartphones have front-facing cameras that work perfectly for checking how I look, a real mirror is not necessary. I prefer to pack light and keep my bag as empty as possible.

- **Pocket mirror** — A tiny mirror you carry in your clothes or bag.
- **Front-facing camera** *(collocation)* — The camera on a phone that points toward the user.
- **Not necessary** — Not needed.

### How often do you use a mirror?

Like most people, I use a mirror several times a day, mostly during my morning routine. I look at my reflection when brushing my teeth, doing my hair, and checking my outfit before leaving the house. Throughout the day, I might quickly glance at one to make sure I look presentable.

- **Morning routine** *(collocation)* — The regular sequence of actions you do each morning to get ready.
- **Look presentable** *(collocation)* — To appear clean, neat, and nice enough for other people to see you.

### Do you like looking at yourself in a mirror?

I wouldn't say I am obsessed with my appearance, but I use mirrors out of practical necessity. It is mostly to make sure my hair isn't messy and my clothes look neat before I meet clients or coworkers. I see it as a basic part of staying clean and polite.

- **Practical necessity** *(collocation)* — Something done because it is useful and required, not for vanity.
- **Meet clients** *(collocation)* — To have professional appointments with business customers.

### Have you ever bought a mirror?

Yes, I recently bought a big, full-length mirror for my bedroom during a home renovation. I spent a lot of time looking for one with a simple metal frame that matches my modern room style. It was a very useful purchase that makes getting dressed much easier.

- **Full-length mirror** *(collocation)* — A tall mirror that shows your entire body.
- **Home renovation** — Improving or repairing a house/apartment.
- **Useful purchase** *(collocation)* — A helpful item that you bought.

### Do you use a mirror before buying clothing?

Absolutely, I would never buy clothes without checking how they look in a fitting room mirror first. Mirrors help me see the fit, length, and style from different angles so I know it looks good on me. It helps me avoid buying things that I will regret later.

- **Fitting room** — A small private cubicle in a clothing store.
- **Different angles** *(collocation)* — Multiple viewpoints or directions.
- **Regret later** — To feel sorry about an action in the future.

### What functions does a mirror have?

Besides its main use for personal grooming and checking your look, a mirror has a few design functions too. In home decoration, it is a great tool to spread natural light and make tiny rooms feel more spacious. Historically, special mirrors have also been essential components of science tools like telescopes.

- **Personal grooming** *(collocation)* — The practice of cleaning and making your physical appearance neat.
- **Natural light** — Light that comes from the sun.
- **Essential components** *(collocation)* — Absolutely necessary parts of a machine or system.

### Do you think a mirror is a good decoration?

Without a doubt, mirrors make great decorations because they look nice and have practical value. Unlike normal paintings, a mirror changes depending on the room and fits into any color scheme by reflecting the space. It is a cheap but effective way to make a home look more elegant.

- **Practical value** *(collocation)* — Useful benefit or utility.
- **Color scheme** *(collocation)* — A planned combination of colors chosen for a room's design.
- **Effective way** — A successful method of doing something.

## 8. Music (May-Dec 2026)

### Do you prefer sad or happy music?

My choice in music changes a lot depending on my emotional state and the time of day. When I need to study or relax at night, I prefer slow, sad songs because they are peaceful and calm. On the other hand, when I am doing physical exercise, I always play happy, fast songs to get moving.

- **Emotional state** *(collocation)* — A person's mood or feelings at a specific time.
- **Physical exercise** *(collocation)* — Workouts, running, or sports done to stay healthy.
- **Fast songs** — Upbeat music tracks.

### Does happy music make you feel more excited?

Yes, fast and cheerful songs have an immediate positive effect on my overall mood. The energetic beat gives me a strong feeling of motivation, making me ready to handle difficult tasks. It works as a quick energy lift whenever I am feeling tired or lazy.

- **Positive effect** — An optimistic or beneficial result.
- **Overall mood** *(collocation)* — The general emotional state of a person.
- **Strong feeling** *(collocation)* — An intense emotion or sensation.

## 9. Tidiness (May-Dec 2026)

### Would you say you are a tidy person?

I would definitely say I am an organized person who cares a lot about cleanliness. I find it hard to focus or relax when my desk or room is messy and full of random objects. Because of this, I spend a few minutes every night cleaning up my workspace.

- **Cleanliness** — The state of being clean and free from dirt.
- **Random objects** *(collocation)* — Unspecified, mixed items cluttering a space.
- **Cleaning up** — Tidying an area.

### Do you like to keep things tidy?

Yes, keeping my room organized gives me a clear mind and helps me feel in control. When everything is in its proper place, I don't waste time looking for lost keys or papers. For me, tidiness is an important part of a stress-free life.

- **Clear mind** — A peaceful mental state free of worry.
- **Proper place** *(collocation)* — The correct or designated spot for an item.
- **Waste time** *(collocation)* — To spend time uselessly.

### Did you use to keep your room tidy as a child?

To be completely honest, I was pretty messy and untidy when I was a little kid. My bedroom floor was always covered in toys, books, and drawing markers, which made my parents highly critical of my mess. It was only when I became a teenager that I realized the importance of being clean.

- **Little kid** — A young child.
- **Highly critical** *(collocation)* — Extremely disapproving or quick to find fault.
- **Importance of being clean** — The value of maintaining neatness.

### How do you keep things tidy?

I stay organized by following a simple rule: putting things back where they belong right after using them. I also do a thorough cleaning session once a month to throw away or donate items I don't need anymore. Using plastic storage boxes also helps keep the mess away.

- **Thorough cleaning** — A deep and complete tidy-up.
- **Donate items** *(collocation)* — To give belongings for free to help charities or poor people.
- **Storage boxes** — Containers used for organizing goods.

### Do you think it is possible for people to be tidy all the time?

No, I think expecting people to be perfectly neat all the time is not realistic given how busy life is. When people are dealing with heavy workloads or family emergencies, cleaning a room becomes a low priority. It is much healthier to try to be clean most of the time instead of perfect.

- **Heavy workloads** *(collocation)* — A massive, stressful amount of work to complete.
- **Family emergencies** *(collocation)* — Sudden, serious unexpected events involving family members.
- **Low priority** — Something that is not urgent or highly important.
