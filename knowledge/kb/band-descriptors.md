# Band descriptors: how Writing and Speaking are marked

Examiners mark Writing and Speaking against **four criteria each**, equally weighted. This page
summarises the public IELTS band descriptors in plain language and focuses on **bands 5–8**, where
the difference between scores usually lies. The app's AI feedback uses the same criteria and
abbreviations.

## Writing

| Code | Criterion | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| **TA** | Task Achievement (Task 1) | covering the key features, a clear **overview**, accurate data, at least 150 words |
| **TR** | Task Response (Task 2) | answering **all parts** of the question, a clear position throughout, developed and supported ideas, at least 250 words |
| **CC** | Coherence & Cohesion | logical organisation, paragraphing, a clear central topic in each paragraph, linking words and reference (*this, such*) used accurately and not mechanically |
| **LR** | Lexical Resource | range and precision of vocabulary, collocation, less common words, spelling and word formation |
| **GRA** | Grammatical Range & Accuracy | a mix of simple and complex sentences, the proportion of error-free sentences, punctuation |

**Scoring:** each task gets a band from the four criteria (averaged). **Task 2 counts twice as much as
Task 1** in the Writing band. Under-length answers are penalised in TA/TR. Off-topic or memorised text is
heavily penalised.

### Task 1: Task Achievement (TA)
| Band | Key features |
|---|---|
| 8 | Covers all requirements; **clearly presents and highlights key features**; well-selected data; occasional omissions only. |
| 7 | Covers the requirements; **clear overview** of main trends/differences/stages; key features highlighted but could be more fully extended. |
| 6 | Addresses the task; an overview is present **but may be unclear**; key features adequately highlighted; some details irrelevant or inaccurate. |
| 5 | Generally addresses the task; **no clear overview**; recounts detail mechanically; may focus on details without data; possible inaccuracies. |

Course rules that map to TA: *Overall,* sentence with **no numbers**, trends and max/min only; detail
paragraphs **with** numbers, percentages and years; one-sentence paraphrased introduction (see
[Task 1 overview](writing-task1-overview.md)).

### Task 2: Task Response (TR)
| Band | Key features |
|---|---|
| 8 | Sufficiently addresses all parts; **well-developed** response with relevant, extended and supported ideas. |
| 7 | Addresses all parts; **clear position throughout**; main ideas extended and supported, though some may be over-generalised. |
| 6 | Addresses all parts, though some more fully than others; relevant position, but the conclusion may be unclear or repetitive; some ideas insufficiently developed. |
| 5 | Addresses the task only partially; position unclear; ideas limited and not well developed; may be repetitive. |

Typical TR losses: answering only one question of a two-part (direct question) essay; giving no
opinion when the question asks for one ("Discuss both views **and give your opinion**"); examples
that are not linked back to the argument.

### Coherence & Cohesion (both tasks)
| Band | Key features |
|---|---|
| 8 | Information sequenced logically; cohesion managed well; paragraphing sufficient and appropriate. |
| 7 | Logical organisation and clear progression; range of cohesive devices, with some under- or over-use; **a clear central topic in each paragraph**. |
| 6 | Coherent overall; cohesive devices effective but cohesion within/between sentences may be faulty or **mechanical**; referencing not always clear. |
| 5 | Some organisation but lacking overall progression; inadequate, inaccurate or over-used linkers; paragraphing may be inadequate. |

### Lexical Resource (Writing)
| Band | Key features |
|---|---|
| 8 | Wide range used fluently and flexibly for precise meanings; skilful use of uncommon items; rare errors in word choice/collocation. |
| 7 | Sufficient range for flexibility and precision; **less common items** with awareness of style and **collocation**; occasional errors in word choice, spelling, word formation. |
| 6 | Adequate range; attempts less common vocabulary with some inaccuracy; errors in spelling/word formation that do not impede communication. |
| 5 | Limited range, minimally adequate; noticeable errors in spelling/word formation that may cause difficulty for the reader. |

### Grammatical Range & Accuracy (Writing)
| Band | Key features |
|---|---|
| 8 | Wide range of structures; **the majority of sentences are error-free**; only very occasional errors. |
| 7 | Variety of complex structures; **frequent error-free sentences**; good control, though a few errors persist. |
| 6 | Mix of simple and complex forms; some errors in grammar and punctuation that rarely reduce communication. |
| 5 | Limited range; complex sentences attempted but less accurate than simple ones; frequent errors; punctuation faulty. |

## Speaking

| Code | Criterion | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| **FC** | Fluency & Coherence | speaking at length without noticeable effort, hesitation, self-correction; logical linking of ideas |
| **LR** | Lexical Resource | range of vocabulary, idiomatic language, **paraphrase** when a word is missing |
| **GRA** | Grammatical Range & Accuracy | range of structures, number of error-free sentences |
| **P** | Pronunciation | intelligibility, word and sentence stress, intonation, connected speech, L1 accent's effect |

| Band | FC | LR | GRA | P |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | Fluent, only occasional repetition/self-correction; hesitation is content-related; coherent and well developed | Wide, flexible; uses less common and **idiomatic** vocabulary skilfully; paraphrases effectively | Wide range used flexibly; **majority error-free**; occasional slips | Wide range of features; sustained flexible use; easy to understand; accent has minimal effect |
| 7 | Speaks at length without noticeable effort; some language-related hesitation; flexible use of connectives | Flexible on a variety of topics; some less common/idiomatic items; aware of collocation, some inappropriate choices | Range of complex structures with some flexibility; **frequently error-free**; some persistent errors | All band-6 features plus some band-8 features |
| 6 | Willing to speak at length, but may lose coherence through repetition, self-correction or hesitation | Wide enough to discuss topics at length; meaning clear despite inappropriacies; generally paraphrases successfully | Mix of simple and complex structures, limited flexibility; frequent errors with complex structures, rarely impede | Range of features with mixed control; generally understood, some mispronunciation reduces clarity |
| 5 | Usually maintains flow but uses repetition, self-correction and/or **slow speech**; overuses certain connectives | Manages familiar and unfamiliar topics with limited flexibility; attempts paraphrase with mixed success | Basic forms reasonably accurate; limited complex structures, usually with errors | All band-4 features and some band-6 features |

The teacher's model answers (see [Speaking Part 1 model answers](speaking-part1-model-answers-1.md)) show the band-8 LR behaviour:
every answer uses 2–3 marked collocations or idioms (*gain practical experience*, *steady job*,
*public opinion*) inside natural sentences.

## Overall band

- Listening and Reading: raw score out of 40 → band (conversion tables).
- **Overall band = average of the four skills, rounded to the nearest half band**: .25 rounds up to .5
  and .75 rounds up to the next whole band (e.g. 6.25 → 6.5, 6.75 → 7.0, 6.1 → 6.0).

## Quick self-check before you submit an essay

- [ ] Task 1: one-sentence paraphrase, an **Overall** paragraph with no numbers, two detail paragraphs with data, 150+ words.
- [ ] Task 2: every part of the question answered; position stated in the intro and repeated in the conclusion.
- [ ] One clear idea per body paragraph: topic sentence → explanation → result → example.
- [ ] Linkers varied and correct (see [Linking words](linking-words.md)), not one at the start of every sentence.
- [ ] 3–5 topic collocations from the [vocabulary bank](vocabulary-for-writing.md).
- [ ] Re-read for agreement (*students **find***), articles, plurals and spelling.
