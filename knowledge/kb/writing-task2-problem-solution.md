# Problem / Solution Essay

## How to recognise it

The question describes a negative situation and asks two questions: **what problems** it causes and **how to solve** them.

- *What problems does this cause? What measures could be taken to solve them?*
- *Discuss the problems and suggest ways that the government and individuals can tackle these problems.*
- *What problems are associated with this? What solutions can you suggest?*

If it asks for **causes** (*Why…? / What are the causes…?*) and solutions, use the [cause/solution plan](writing-task2-cause-solution.md).

## Paragraph plan (teacher's "THE IELTS TASK 2: PROBLEM/SOLUTION STRUCTURE")

**1. INTRODUCTION**
- **Sentence 1: Paraphrase the topic.** Rewrite the topic statement in your own words using synonyms.
- **Sentence 2: Thesis statement.** Clearly state the main problem and the main solution you will discuss.

**2. BODY PARAGRAPH 1: THE PROBLEM**
- **Sentence 1: Topic sentence (introduce the problem).** State the main problem.
- **Sentences 2 and 3: Explanation.** Explain how or why this leads to the problem. Go deeper into detail.
- **Sentence 4: The result.** Show the direct consequence or outcome of this.
- **Sentence 5: Real-world example.** Provide a short, specific example (mentioning a country or organization) that proves your point.

**3. BODY PARAGRAPH 2: THE SOLUTION**
- **Sentence 1: Topic sentence (introduce the solution).** State the main action that needs to be taken to fix the problem.
- **Sentences 2 and 3: Explanation.** Explain exactly how this solution works and who should implement it (e.g. governments, schools, individuals).
- **Sentence 4: The result.** Show the positive outcome or change that happens once this solution is applied.
- **Sentence 5: Real-world example.** Provide a short, matching example of a country that successfully used this solution and saw a drop in the problem.

**4. CONCLUSION**
- **Sentence 1: Restate the thesis / summary.** Summarise the main problem and the main solution in one final sentence.

*(The handout's thesis and conclusion lines say "main cause" because they were copied from the cause/solution sheet. In a problem/solution essay, read them as "main problem".)*

## Model essay 1: Several solutions from different groups

**Question:** *More and more plastic waste is polluting the world's cities, countrysides and oceans. What problems does this cause? What measures could be taken to solve them?*

> It is undeniably true that human beings are throwing away more and more plastic waste into the natural world today. This worrying trend has created serious environmental problems across the globe, making it absolutely necessary for national governments to work closely together with ordinary citizens to reduce the long-term damage.
>
> On the one hand, the rising amount of plastic waste creates several predictable problems for our planet. The main issue is that plastic does not break down naturally over time, which poses a permanent and severe threat to sea creatures. A growing number of large marine animals like whales die after accidentally eating plastic waste because their bodies simply cannot digest it. Furthermore, this plastic problem damages the wider environment by heavily polluting vital rivers and oceans, which ultimately harms wildlife and ruins natural habitats. For instance, reports from the World Wildlife Fund (WWF) show that thousands of marine animals, including whales and sea turtles, die every year after accidentally ingesting plastic debris that they cannot digest.
>
> There are practical steps that can be taken by different groups to solve these issues successfully. First, governments should launch national campaigns to encourage people to join environmental activities, which raises public awareness by teaching citizens the long-term impact of their daily waste habits. Second, teamwork between authorities and private businesses can reduce plastic use by actively investing in alternative materials, providing consumers with eco-friendly choices that do not harm the planet. Finally, states must pass stricter laws to limit plastic production, as legal boundaries force manufacturers to stop creating harmful materials altogether. A prime example of this joint effort is seen in the European Union, where member states successfully banned single-use plastics, forcing businesses to switch to paper alternatives and encouraging citizens to adopt reusable bags.
>
> In conclusion, environmental damage and high cleanup costs are the main impacts of plastic pollution today. Overcoming this global problem requires immediate, combined efforts from individual citizens, businesses, and governments alike.

**Analysis**

- *Problems:* plastic does not break down, which kills marine animals, and it pollutes rivers and oceans. The example is the **WWF**.
- *Solutions:* three steps for three groups (governments run campaigns, authorities and businesses invest in alternatives, states pass stricter laws), with one matching example: the **EU ban on single-use plastics**.

## Model essay 2: One main solution, developed in depth

**Question:** *Overpopulation of urban areas has led to numerous problems. Discuss the problems and suggest ways that the government and individuals can tackle these problems.*

> Overpopulation in cities has become a serious issue, putting a heavy strain on government resources and public systems. While this trend causes major problems with severe air and water pollution, the most effective solution is for governments to move people out of crowded cities by developing rural areas.
>
> The main result of overpopulation is the huge rise in pollution across city areas. As the number of people grows, the amount of daily trash and car exhaust quickly becomes too much for city systems to handle. The most immediate danger comes from car smoke, as millions of residents use cars and motorbikes to get to work, which fills the air with dirty gas and thick smog. Additionally, crowded cities struggle to manage household and factory waste. When local systems cannot keep up with the massive amount of garbage made every day, dirty waste and sewage often end up in local rivers, ruining clean water sources and killing local wildlife. A clear example of this is New Delhi, where overcrowding has caused dangerous winter smog and severe river pollution.
>
> However, the best way to solve these pollution problems is for governments to lower the number of people living in big cities. Governments can do this by spending money to build up rural areas and smaller towns, making sure they have good schools, modern hospitals, and fast internet. Furthermore, the government can offer lower taxes and cheaper land to companies that choose to open their offices or factories outside of big cities. When good jobs and great public services are available in the countryside, many citizens will naturally choose to move away from crowded zones, which quickly cuts down city pollution. A prime example of this strategy is South Korea, which successfully moved thousands of workers out of crowded Seoul by building a completely new city called Sejong.
>
> In conclusion, although overpopulation creates difficult challenges like severe air and water pollution, smart government actions can solve these issues. By improving smaller towns and helping people move out of crowded places, governments can clean up city environments and make life better across the country.

*(350 words)*

**Analysis**

- *Problem:* pollution (car smoke and smog, waste and sewage in rivers). The example is **New Delhi**.
- *Solution:* develop rural areas and give tax incentives so that people leave big cities. The matching example is **South Korea's** new city of **Sejong**.
- This essay follows the 5-sentence formula more closely: one problem, one solution, each with explanation, result and example.

## Notes

- The solution paragraph should say **who** acts (*governments, businesses, schools, individuals*) and **how**.
- The solution example should **match** the problem. It shows a place where the solution worked and the problem fell.
- If the question names actors (*"the government and individuals"*), mention those actors in your solutions.
