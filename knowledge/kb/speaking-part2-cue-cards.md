# Speaking Part 2 Cue Cards (May-Dec 2026)

The six cue cards the teacher worked through in class (Topic 1–6 handouts, sent 7 and 19 August), each with the topic vocabulary she grouped into idioms, collocations, phrasal verbs and advanced words. The handouts give no Part 2 sample answer; their Part 3 follow-up questions with model answers are in [speaking-part3-discussion.md](speaking-part3-discussion.md).

**Routine for every card** (1 minute to prepare, speak for up to 2 minutes): write one or two words per bullet point, pick 3–4 phrases from the lists below to use, then answer the bullets in order and finish with the *why / how you felt* point — that last point is where you can talk longest.


## Describe a place you'd like to visit in your free time

Season: **May-Dec 2026** · Handout: *Topic 1 - Travel - P2_3 vocabulary.pdf*

You should say:
- where it is
- what you will do there
- how long you will stay there
- why you'd like to visit it

**Idioms**

- **To escape the hustle and bustle** — To get away from the noisy, busy pace of daily life.
- **To recharge one's batteries** — To regain energy after a period of hard work.
- **To watch the world go by** — To sit and observe people as they move around.
- **To lose track of time** — To forget what time it is because you are fully focused.
- **To feast one's eyes on** — To look at something with great pleasure or delight.
- **To clear one's mind** — To stop worrying and think clearly.
- **To put one's feet up** — To relax completely and take a break.
- **To take a stroll down memory lane** — To look back at past memories or history.
- **To get away from it all** — To take a holiday to escape a busy routine.

**Collocations**

- **An urban oasis** — A peaceful green area inside a busy city.
- **Picturesque scenery** — Very beautiful, visually attractive natural views.
- **To broaden one's horizons** — To expand your knowledge and experiences.
- **To steep oneself in history** — To completely immerse oneself in a historical setting.
- **Breathtaking views** — Extremely striking, beautiful, or surprising sights.
- **Leisure activities** — Things you do for enjoyment when you are not working.
- **A tranquil atmosphere** — A very calm, quiet, and peaceful mood or environment.
- **Vibrant nightlife** — Active, and exciting entertainment options in the evening.
- **A hidden gem** — A beautiful or special place that few people know about.
- **To unwind and de-stress** — To relax and let go of mental tension.

**Essential Everyday Phrasal Verbs**

- **To head down** — To travel or walk towards a specific destination.
- **To meet up** — To gather or assemble with friends at a designated location.
- **To chill out** — To thoroughly relax and do nothing stressful.
- **To look around** — To walk through a place to see what is there.
- **To drop by / in** — To pay a brief, casual, or unplanned visit to a place.
- **To sit back** — To relax in a chair and comfortably watch something happen.
- **To wind down** — To gradually relax after a period of stress or hard work.
- **To clear out** — To leave a place completely, or to empty a space out.
- **To wander around** — To walk slowly through an area without a fixed direction.
- **To stroll along** — To walk in a slow, relaxed, and leisurely way.

**High-Level Vocabulary**

- **Architectural** — Relating to the stunning design and structure of buildings.
- **Geothermal** — Relating to the natural internal heat of the earth.
- **Awe-inspiring** — Causing an overwhelming feeling of deep respect, or wonder.
- **Metropolitan** — Relating to a very large, dense, and busy city.
- **Panoramic** — Offering a wide and spectacular view of an entire surrounding area.
- **Rustic** — Having a charming, simple, and rough beauty typical of the countryside.
- **Facade** — The principal front face of a building that looks onto a street.
- **Topography** — The physical arrangement of natural and artificial features of an area.
- **To captivate** — To attract and hold the intense interest and attention of someone.
- **To replicate** — To make an exact copy, reproduce, or mimic a specific feeling.
- **To alleviate** — To make physical or mental stress less severe or more bearable.
- **To illuminate** — To light up a space or make something highly visible.
- **To elevate** — To raise something to a much higher or more impressive level.
- **To encapsulate** — To express the essential features of something in a brief way.
- **To navigate** — To find your way through a complex or unfamiliar area.
- **To indulge** — To allow yourself to freely enjoy a particular pleasure.


## Describe a tall building in your town that you like or dislike

Season: **May-Dec 2026** · Handout: *Topic 2 - Tall building - P2_3 vocabulary.pdf*

You should say:
- where this building is
- what it looks like
- what it is used for
- explain why you like or dislike it

**Idioms**

- **To reach for the sky** — To be exceptionally tall or ambitious in design.
- **To break the bank** — To be incredibly expensive to construct.
- **To have your head in the clouds** — To be so tall that the top floors are often surrounded by fog or mist.
- **To be a sight for sore eyes** — A building that is exceptionally beautiful or pleasant to look at.
- **To be history in the making** — A new building that will become an important historic landmark in the future.
- **To be a hard pill to swallow** — A building project that citizens dislike but have to accept.
- **To stand out from the crowd** — To be vastly different and more noticeable than surrounding structures.
- **To hold its own** — To look good and maintain its dignity next to much newer, bigger buildings.

**Collocations**

- **A soaring skyscraper** — A very tall, impressive modern building.
- **A dominant feature** — A structure that instantly catches your eye in the city skyline.
- **State-of-the-art architecture** — Extremely modern, high-tech building design.
- **An architectural marvel** — A building that is a masterpiece of engineering and design.
- **A sprawling complex** — A very large building that covers a massive ground area.
- **A striking contrast** — A building that looks completely different from the structures next to it.
- **A multi-purpose facility** — A building used for many different things like shopping, offices, and living.
- **A dilapidated structure** — A building that is old, broken, and in very poor condition.
- **An eco-friendly design** — A building made to protect the environment using green technology.
- **The focal point** — The center of interest or attention in a neighborhood.

**High-Level Phrasal Verbs**

- **To tower above / over** — To stand much taller than the surrounding buildings or trees.
- **To pull down / tear down** — To completely demolish an old or unwanted building.
- **To block out** — To stop natural sunlight from reaching the streets below.
- **To blend in with** — To match the style and appearance of the surrounding neighborhood perfectly.
- **To stand out against** — To be easily seen because of a contrasting background like the sky.
- **To fit out** — To decorate and provide a building with necessary furniture and equipment.
- **To check out** — To visit a building to look at its design or features.

**Adjectives**

- **Monolithic** — Massive, solid, and uniform in structure.
- **Futuristic** — Looking like it belongs to a time far in the future.
- **Intimidating** — Looking cold, scary, or too powerful.
- **Ornate** — Highly decorated with complex, beautiful carvings or patterns.
- **Aesthetically pleasing** — Beautiful and very satisfying to look at.
- **Sleek** — Smooth, shiny, elegant, and modern.
- **Ostentatious** — Too expensive and showy, built just to impress people.
- **Quaint** — Charmingly old-fashioned or unusual in a nice way.
- **Imposing** — Very large and impressive in appearance.


## Describe an occasion when you got up extremely early

Season: **May-Dec 2026** · Handout: *Topic 3 - Getting up early Vocabulary.pdf*

You should say:
- when this happened
- what you needed to do on that day
- who you were with
- how you felt about getting up early on that day

**Idioms**

- **At the crack of dawn** — At the very first light of daybreak.
- **The early bird catches the worm** — Success comes to those who start early.
- **Bright and early** — Very early in the morning, feeling fresh.
- **An early riser** — A person who consistently enjoys waking up early.
- **A night owl** — Someone who prefers staying up late over waking up early.

**Collocations**

- **Morning ritual** — A set of habits practiced right after waking up.
- **Productive window** — A timeframe in the morning with high focus.
- **Snooze button** — The alarm feature used to delay waking up.
- **Peaceful atmosphere** — The quiet environment of the world at dawn.
- **Strict discipline** — The willpower needed to wake up early consistently.

**Advanced Words**

- **Tranquility** — The quality or state of being calm and peaceful.
- **Lethargic** — Feeling sluggish, lazy, and completely lacking energy.
- **Rejuvenated** — Feeling completely refreshed and energized after sleep.
- **Solitude** — The state of being completely alone in the quiet morning.
- **Regimen** — A strict daily routine of habits or exercise.


## Describe a person you know who loves to grow plants (vegetables, fruits, flowers)

Season: **May-Dec 2026** · Handout: *Topic 4 - Planting Vocabulary.pdf*

You should say:
- who this person is
- what this person grows
- where this person grows them
- why this person loves to grow plants

**Idioms**

- **Green thumb** — A natural talent for growing plants successfully.
- **Bear fruit** — To produce good results or actual fruits/vegetables.
- **Nip it in the bud** — To stop a problem at the very beginning.
- **Late bloomer** — A plant (or person) that grows or succeeds later than others.
- **No bed of roses** — A difficult situation that requires a lot of hard work.

**Collocations**

- **Fertile soil** — Rich, healthy dirt that helps plants grow well.
- **Bountiful harvest** — A large, successful crop of fruits or vegetables.
- **Hardy species** — Strong plants that can survive tough weather.
- **Morning ritual** — A daily routine, like watering plants early in the day.
- **Nurturing environment** — A space with the perfect amount of sun and water.

**Advanced Words**

- **Cultivate** — To prepare land and care for plants as they grow.
- **Germinate** — The moment a tiny seed starts to sprout into a plant.
- **Flora** — A formal or scientific word for plant life.
- **Tranquility** — A deep sense of peace and quiet found in a garden.
- **Solitude** — The state of being peacefully alone outdoors with nature.


## Describe a friend from your childhood

Season: **May-Dec 2026** · Handout: *Topic 5 - Friend Vocabulary.pdf*

You should say:
- who this person is
- how you met each other
- what you did together
- explain why you liked him or her

**Idioms (Band 8+)**

- **Chalk and cheese** — Completely different people.
- **Hit it off** — Formed an immediate bond.
- **Thicker than water** — Stronger than other relationships.
- **A kindred spirit** — Someone with identical values.
- **A shoulder to cry on** — A sympathetic listener.
- **Fair-weather friend** — A friend only in success.
- **Speak the same language** — Having identical understandings.
- **See eye to eye** — Agreeing completely with someone.
- **Cross paths** — To meet by chance.
- **Clear the air** — Resolving a past misunderstanding.
- **A lifelong confidant** — A trusted secret keeper.
- **Get on like a house on fire** — Becoming friends incredibly fast.

**Collocations (Band 8+)**

- **Unshakeable bond** — An unbreakable emotional connection.
- **Indelible impression** — A permanent impact on mind.
- **Shared history** — A common past together.
- **Unwavering loyalty** — Constant, unchanging support over time.
- **Childhood camaraderie** — Trust built during youth.
- **Nostalgic reflection** — Thinking fondly about the past.
- **Deep-seated affection** — A profound, long-lasting love.
- **Fostered friendship** — A relationship nurtured over time.
- **Boyhood/Girlhood companion** — A friend from early youth.
- **Emotional anchor** — Someone providing stability and support.
- **Enduring attachment** — A long-lasting emotional connection.
- **Harmonious relationship** — A peaceful, conflict-free bond.

**High-Advanced Words (Band 8+)**

- **Unprecedented** — Never done or known before.
- **Inextricable** — Impossible to separate or untangle.
- **Affinity** — A natural liking or sympathy.
- **Convivial** — Cheerful, friendly, and lively atmosphere.
- **Altruistic** — Showing unselfish concern for others.
- **Evanescent** — Soon passing out of sight.
- **Perennial** — Lasting for an indefinitely long time.
- **Immutable** — Unchanging over a long period.
- **Equanimity** — Mental calmness in difficult situations.
- **Anecdote** — A short, amusing real story.
- **Propinquity** — State of being close to someone.
- **Veritable** — Used to highlight a metaphor.


## Describe a plan you had to change

Season: **May-Dec 2026** · Handout: *Topic 6 - Plan Vocabulary.pdf*

You should say:
- what the plan was
- why you had to change it
- what the new plan was
- how you felt about the change

**Idioms**

- **Play it by ear** — Decide as the situation develops.
- **At the eleventh hour** — At the last possible moment.
- **Call a rain check** — Postpone a plan for later.
- **Up in the air** — Uncertain or not decided yet.
- **Change of heart** — A shift in your decision.

**Collocations**

- **Last-minute alteration** — A small change right before an event.
- **Contingency plan** — A backup plan if things fail.
- **Schedule conflict** — A clash between two timed events.
- **Spontaneous decision** — A choice made with no preparation.
- **Sudden detour** — An abrupt change in your route.

**Advanced Words**

- **Reschedule** — To change the date or time.
- **Improvise** — To make do without prior preparation.
- **Spontaneous** — Done naturally without planning ahead.
- **Pragmatic** — Being realistic about necessary changes.
- **Haphazard** — Messy and completely lacking any organization.
