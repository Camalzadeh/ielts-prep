# Linking Words for IELTS Writing

Linking words (cohesive devices) are part of the **Coherence and Cohesion** score. This page has the teacher's *Linking Words* handout, organised by function, plus the linkers that appear again and again in her Task 2 and Task 1 model answers, with real example sentences.

---

## 1. The teacher's handout (by function)

| Function | Linking words |
|---|---|
| **Listing** | Firstly · Secondly · Thirdly · Fourthly · Lastly, Finally / Last but not least |
| **Adding information** | Additionally · Furthermore · In addition to · Not only… but also · Moreover · As well as |
| **Giving examples** | For example · For instance · To cite an example · To show an example · To illustrate · One clear example is |
| **Results and consequences** | Therefore · Thus · As a result · Consequently · Hence · As a consequence |
| **Highlighting and stressing** | Specifically · Particularly · Especially · In particular · Obviously |
| **Concessions and contrasts** | However · While · Whereas · Although · On the other hand · Nevertheless · Despite · Even though · By contrast · In spite of · In comparison with |
| **Causes and reasons** | Owing to · Because · Due to · Since · As |
| **Presenting your opinion** | I believe · I admit · I think · In my opinion · In my view · I agree / concur · I disagree with / I cannot accept |
| **Conclusion linkers** | To conclude · In conclusion · In summary · To summarize |

## 2. Grammar: how each linker is used

Many linking mistakes are **grammar** mistakes. Check what comes after the linker.

| Pattern | Linkers | Example (from the teacher's models) |
|---|---|---|
| **Linker + comma + full sentence** (starts a new sentence) | However, · Furthermore, · Moreover, · Additionally, · Consequently, · Therefore, · As a result, · Nevertheless, · By contrast, · On the other hand, · For instance, · For example, · To illustrate, | *However, this benefit is weak because television remains a completely passive learning medium…* |
| **Linker + clause** (joins two clauses in one sentence) | while · whereas · although · even though · because · since · as | *…whereas others contend that commercial enterprises derive the greatest benefit.* |
| **Linker + noun / -ing** (NOT a full clause) | despite · in spite of · due to · owing to · in addition to · as well as · in comparison with | *…despite working in dangerous areas.* / *…due to the toxic gas emissions generated by daily human activities.* |
| **Linker + noun phrase + , + result** | As a result of … , · As a consequence of … , | *As a result of these low salaries, they feel unhappy…* / *As a consequence of these higher salaries, they feel valued…* |
| **Paired** | Not only … but also … · On the one hand … On the other hand … | *This problem not only ruins the physical health of individuals but also places a massive financial strain on medical systems…* |

Common errors to avoid:
- ✗ *Despite it was expensive…* → ✓ *Despite **being** expensive…* / ✓ *Although it was expensive…*
- ✗ *Due to people work long hours…* → ✓ *Due to **long working hours**…* / ✓ *Because people work long hours…*
- ✗ *On the other hand* used just to add a point → it must show a **contrasting** side.
- ✗ Starting every sentence with a linker. Use them where the logic changes, not in every sentence.

## 3. Linkers by essay part (as used in the Task 2 model essays)

| Where | Linkers the teacher uses |
|---|---|
| **Introduction** | *While …, I believe …* · *whereas others contend that …* · *This essay will examine how … and explain why …* |
| **Starting Body 1** | *To begin with,* · *First,* · *On the one hand,* · *The primary reason why …* |
| **Starting Body 2** | *Furthermore,* · *Second,* · *Similarly,* · *On the other hand,* · *Conversely,* · *However,* · *Nevertheless, despite …* · *Instead of …,* |
| **Adding a second point inside a paragraph** | *Furthermore,* · *Additionally,* · *In addition,* · *Finally,* · *The second major cause is …* · *The second step is …* · *Firstly, … Secondly, …* |
| **Explaining a result** | *As a result,* · *Consequently,* · *Therefore,* · *This means that …* · *which naturally leads to …* · *Eventually,* |
| **Introducing the example** | *For example,* · *For instance,* · *To illustrate,* · *A clear example of this is …* · *A prime example of this …* · *A clear illustration of this …* |
| **Conclusion** | *In conclusion, while / although …* · *Ultimately, I maintain that …* · *However, this issue can be solved if …* |

## 4. Linkers for Writing Task 1

**Comparing and contrasting data** (from the Task 1 model answers)

| Linker | Example |
|---|---|
| *while / whereas* (overview!) | *Overall, while the proportion of immigrants from the UK declined steadily throughout the period, the figures for Asia and other regions generally increased.* |
| *In contrast, / By contrast,* | *By contrast, immigration from Asia rose steadily from 6% in 1976 to around 10% in 1996…* |
| *By comparison,* | *By comparison, exports to China began at a very low level of around 1 billion in 1990…* |
| *Meanwhile,* | *Meanwhile, exports to the US fluctuated.* |
| *Similarly, / A similar pattern was observed…* | *Similarly, exports to India increased steadily…* |
| *In contrast to …,* | *In contrast to seals and whales, the dolphin population experienced substantial growth…* |
| *However,* | *However, this figure decreased consistently over the period…* |
| *Although …,* | *Although the proportion of commuters taking the bus stood lower than that of cars in 2004…* |
| *Regarding … / In terms of … / When it comes to … / Looking at …* (opening a body paragraph) | *Regarding the remaining categories, …* · *In terms of Eldan Ltd, …* · *When it comes to people aged 40-59, …* · *Looking at the destinations in 1999, …* |
| *respectively* | *…making roughly $30,000 and $25,000 respectively.* |
| *Notably,* | *Notably, the dolphin population surpassed both seals and whales by 2018…* |
| *In addition, / Additionally,* (second overview sentence) | *In addition, at the beginning, Japan was the main importer…* |
