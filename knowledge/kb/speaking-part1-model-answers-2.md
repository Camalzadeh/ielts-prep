**How the model answers are built** — almost every answer is three sentences:
1. a direct answer to the question (often with a softener such as *To be completely honest*, *Generally speaking*, *Without a doubt*);
2. a reason or a concrete detail;
3. an extra point, example or contrast (*Also*, *Plus*, *However*, *On top of that*).

Practise by answering aloud in your own words (20–30 seconds each) and try to reuse two or three of the listed collocations — do not memorise the answers word for word; examiners notice rehearsed speech.

## 10. Websites (May-Dec 2026)

### What kinds of websites do you often visit?

On a daily basis, I visit learning websites, news portals, and professional networking platforms like LinkedIn. I use these sites to stay updated on global news and to learn new skills for my job. Sometimes, I also visit streaming sites or online shops for fun.

- **Daily basis** — Every day.
- **Networking platforms** — Websites designed to connect people professionally or socially.
- **Global news** *(collocation)* — News events happening all around the world.

### What kinds of websites are popular in your country?

In my country, social media platforms and large online marketplaces are definitely the most popular. Sites like Instagram and local e-commerce stores get millions of visitors every single day. Also, news websites and online banking are widely used by almost everyone.

- **Online marketplaces** — Websites where multiple merchants sell products.
- **E-commerce stores** *(collocation)* — Websites designed for buying and selling retail products online.
- **Online banking** — Managing your bank accounts over the internet.

### What is your favourite website?

If I had to choose only one, my absolute favorite site would be YouTube because of its endless educational content. Whether I want to learn a computer language or try a new recipe, there is always a free guide available. It is a great site that combines entertainment with learning.

- **Educational content** *(collocation)* — Videos, text, or audio that teaches you something useful.
- **Computer language** — Coding scripts like Python or Java.
- **New recipe** *(collocation)* — A new set of instructions for cooking a specific dish.

### Are there any changes to the websites you often visit?

Yes, I have noticed that the sites I use are starting to add AI features to show customized content. The layouts are also becoming simpler, with popular options like dark mode to protect your eyes. While some updates are easy to use, others take a bit of time to learn.

- **AI features** — Artificial intelligence tools.
- **Customized content** — Content tailored to your specific history.
- **Dark mode** *(collocation)* — A computer screen setting that displays light text on a dark background.

## 11. Watches (May-Dec 2026)

### Do you like to wear watches?

Yes, I am a big fan of watches and consider them an important accessory for my outfits. Wearing a wristwatch feels much more professional than pulling out a phone just to check the time. It adds a nice, neat touch to both casual clothes and business suits.

- **Big fan** — Someone who likes or admires something very much.
- **Wristwatch** — A watch worn around the wrist.
- **Casual clothes** *(collocation)* — Comfortable, informal everyday clothing.

### Do you think a watch is important for you?

Absolutely, a watch is an essential tool for me because it helps me manage my day well. It serves as a gentle reminder of the passing hours, which helps me arrive on time for meetings and appointments. Besides telling the time, it is also a way to show my personal style.

- **Essential tool** — A highly necessary device or object.
- **Arrive on time** *(collocation)* — To reach a destination exactly at the planned hour without being late.
- **Personal style** *(collocation)* — An individual's unique taste in clothes and accessories.

### Have you ever received a watch as a gift?

Yes, on my twentieth birthday, my parents gave me a beautiful leather-strap watch. It is a very elegant piece that has my name engraved on the back of it. Because it was for a special milestone, I care about it a lot and only wear it for special events.

- **Leather-strap watch** — A watch with a band made of leather material.
- **Special milestone** *(collocation)* — A highly significant or important event in life (like turning 20 or graduating).
- **Special events** — Celebrations like weddings or parties.

### Why do people like expensive watches?

Many people buy luxury watches because they are seen as status symbols that show wealth and success. Also, these pricey watches are famous for their excellent quality, fine materials, and long durability. For some collectors, they are even seen as smart investments that gain value over time.

- **Luxury watches** — High-end, expensive watches.
- **Status symbols** — Items that represent high social standing.
- **Excellent quality** *(collocation)* — A very high standard of craftsmanship or value.

## 12. Shopping (May-Dec 2026)

### Do you like shopping?

Generally, I find shopping to be a fun activity, especially when I am looking for new clothes or books. However, I prefer online shopping over going to physical stores because it helps me avoid crowded places and long lines. It can be a very relaxing experience if I don't spend too much money.

- **Fun activity** — An enjoyable pastime.
- **Physical stores** *(collocation)* — Brick-and-mortar retail shops you can physically walk into.
- **Long lines** — Queues of waiting people.

### How often do you go shopping?

I usually go on a big shopping trip about once a month, mostly to buy clothes or household items. For basic groceries and fresh food, I make quick visits to the local supermarket every week. I try to avoid frequent shopping trips to save money and stop unplanned buying.

- **Shopping trip** — A planned journey to buy goods.
- **Household items** — Goods used inside a home (like cleaning spray or soap).
- **Basic groceries** *(collocation)* — Essential food items needed for cooking (like milk, bread, or eggs).

### Do you compare prices when you shop?

Yes, I am a very budget-conscious shopper, so I always check prices across different stores before buying. Thanks to mobile apps, comparing costs online has become incredibly quick and easy. Doing this extra research ensures that I always get the best value for my money.

- **Budget-conscious** — Careful with spending money.
- **Mobile apps** — Software on smart phones.
- **Best value** *(collocation)* — The most optimal benefit or quality for the price paid.

### Is it difficult for you to make choices when you shop?

I have to admit that I often find it hard to choose because stores offer too many options available. When I see dozens of items that look the same and have positive reviews, picking just one is very hard. I often have to watch online videos to help me make a decision.

- **Too many options** — An overwhelming variety of choices.
- **Positive reviews** *(collocation)* — Good comments or star-ratings left by previous buyers.
- **Make a decision** *(collocation)* — To choose a final option after thinking about it.

## 13. Cars (May-Dec 2026)

### What type of car do you like?

I am really interested in modern electric cars and compact SUVs. I like electric vehicles because they protect the environment, run quietly, and have advanced technology. Compact SUVs are also great because they are strong and practical for city streets.

- **Protect the environment** — To act in ways that keep nature clean and safe.
- **Advanced technology** *(collocation)* — Highly modern, cutting-edge engineering or electronic tools.
- **City streets** — Roads inside an urban area.

### What colour car would you choose to buy?

If I were to buy a car tomorrow, I would definitely choose a cool metallic gray or a classic black. These colors look very professional, timeless, and don't show dirt or scratches too easily. Plus, neutral colors are usually easier to sell later on.

- **Metallic gray** — A shiny gray color that looks like polished steel.
- **Timeless** — Always fashionable; never getting old.
- **Neutral colors** *(collocation)* — Non-vivid colors like white, black, or gray.

### Do you think car colours are important?

Yes, I think a car's color matters because it shows a bit of the owner's style and personality. From a safety perspective, bright colors are much easier to see on the road at night, which can help prevent accidents. Also, dark cars absorb heat, making them hotter in sunny weather.

- **Matters** — Is important.
- **Safety perspective** *(collocation)* — Looking at a situation from a viewpoint of avoiding danger.
- **Prevent accidents** — To stop vehicle crashes or mishaps from happening.

### What do you usually do when there's a traffic jam?

Whenever I get stuck in heavy traffic, I pass the time by listening to interesting podcasts or audiobooks. It really helps distract me from feeling angry about the delay and turns boring waiting time into a learning experience. If I am with friends, we just play music and talk to stay happy.

- **Heavy traffic** *(collocation)* — A massive number of cars moving very slowly on a road.
- **Pass the time** — To do something to make waiting hours go faster.
- **Learning experience** *(collocation)* — An event or activity where you acquire new knowledge.

### Do you prefer to be a driver or a passenger?

I much prefer sitting in the passenger seat because I can relax without the stress of driving through busy traffic. As a passenger, I can look at the view, reply to text messages, or even take a nap. Driving requires continuous focus, which I find quite tiring.

- **Passenger seat** — The car seat where you ride without driving.
- **Take a nap** *(collocation)* — To sleep for a short period of time during the day.
- **Continuous focus** — Non-stop concentration on a task.

## 14. Parks (May-Dec 2026)

### Do you like to go to parks?

Yes, I really love going to public parks because they offer a beautiful green space away from concrete city streets. I like to go there to escape the loud traffic noise and get some fresh air under the trees. It is the perfect place to clear my mind and relax on a nice afternoon.

- **Public parks** — Green park areas owned by the city for everyone to use.
- **Traffic noise** *(collocation)* — The loud sounds made by cars, trucks, and horns on roads.
- **Clear my mind** — To remove stress and think clearly by relaxing.

### When was the last time you went to the park?

The last time I went to a park was just this past Saturday morning for an early jog with a friend. The weather conditions were very nice and sunny, which brought out a lot of families and people walking their dogs. We exercised for an hour and then sat on a bench to talk.

- **Early jog** — A slow, steady run done early in the morning.
- **Weather conditions** *(collocation)* — The state of the atmosphere (rainy, sunny, windy, cold) at a specific time.
- **Walk their dogs** — To take dogs outside for exercise.

### Do people in your country often go to parks?

Absolutely, public parks are popular gathering places for people of all ages in my country. In the morning, you will see senior citizens doing light exercises, while the evenings attract joggers and families. They are important spots for relaxing, especially for people who live in small flats.

- **Gathering places** — Locations where people meet up in groups.
- **All ages** — People of any generation (young and old).
- **Senior citizens** *(collocation)* — Elderly or older people, usually retired.

## 15. Science (May-Dec 2026)

### Do you like science?

Yes, I really appreciate science because it gives clear, logical answers for how the universe works. I am especially interested in recent discoveries in medicine and space travel, which are changing our future. It is an amazing subject that always helps humans learn and grow.

- **Logical answers** — Explanations based on clear reasoning and facts.
- **Recent discoveries** *(collocation)* — New findings or inventions made by scientists short ago.
- **Space travel** — Traveling outside of planet Earth into the universe.

### Did you like science classes when you were young?

To be honest, I found science classes a bit boring in primary school because we just had to memorize facts. My mind changed in high school when we started doing scientific experiments in the lab. Seeing real chemical reactions made the lessons exciting and made me very curious.

- **Primary school** — Elementary school for young children.
- **Scientific experiments** *(collocation)* — Practical tests done in a laboratory to prove scientific theories.
- **Chemical reactions** — The process where substances mix and transform into new forms.

## 16. Outer Space and Stars (May-Dec 2026)

### Do you want to travel in the outer space?

I would absolutely jump at the chance to travel into the cosmos if the opportunity arose. The idea of experiencing zero gravity and floating above the Earth sounds completely exhilarating. However, I do have some reservations about the intense physical toll and the astronomical costs involved in space tourism. Ultimately, the thrill of witnessing the vast expanse of the universe firsthand would definitely outweigh my fears.

- **Zero gravity** — A weightless state where gravity is not felt.
- **Exhilarating** — Incredibly exciting and thrilling.
- **Astronomical costs** — Extremely high and massive prices.
- **Vast expanse** — A huge, endless open area.

### What would you do if you had an opportunity?

If I were given the chance to venture into space, my absolute priority would be to look out the window and marvel at the panoramic view of Earth. I would definitely want to document the entire experience by capturing breathtaking photos of distant galaxies and nebulae. Additionally, I would love to try standard activities like eating or moving around to see how they feel in a weightless environment. It would truly be a once-in-a-lifetime experience that I would cherish forever.

- **Panoramic view** — A wide, uninterrupted view of an entire area.
- **Breathtaking photos** — Beautiful and astonishing pictures.
- **Weightless environment** — A setting where things float freely.
- **Once-in-a-lifetime experience** — A very rare and special event.

### Do you think it's necessary to see other planets?

Yes, I believe exploring other planets is highly beneficial for the advancement of human knowledge. Sending probes or astronauts to uncharted territories helps us understand the origins of our own solar system and search for potential signs of life. Furthermore, studying the atmospheric conditions of neighboring planets can provide invaluable insights into how we can better preserve Earth. It expands our horizons and ensures the long-term survival of our species.

- **Uncharted territories** — Completely unknown and unexplored places.
- **Potential signs of life** — Clues suggesting living things exist.
- **Invaluable insights** — Extremely useful and precious knowledge.
- **Long-term survival** — Continuing to exist safely over time.

### Are you interested in films about outer space and stars?

I am incredibly captivated by films that feature interstellar travel and cosmic phenomena. Movies that depict the sheer scale of the universe always fill me with a deep sense of wonder and awe. I especially appreciate when filmmakers rely on scientific accuracy to create realistic depictions of black holes or distant worlds. They are not just visually spectacular but also thought-provoking.

- **Sheer scale** — The massive size or immense magnitude.
- **Cosmic phenomena** — Natural events happening in outer space.
- **Scientific accuracy** — Being completely correct according to science.
- **Thought-provoking** — Making you think deeply about a topic.

### Do you like science fiction movies?

Sci-fi is actually one of my absolute favorite film genres to watch. I find it fascinating how these movies explore futuristic technologies and alternative realities that stretch the boundaries of our imagination. They often serve as a social commentary, raising important questions about where our current scientific advancements might lead us. It is the perfect blend of mind-bending concepts and thrilling entertainment.

- **Futuristic technologies** — Highly advanced tools of the future.
- **Social commentary** — Artwork that critiques issues in society.
- **Scientific advancements** — Modern breakthroughs and developments in science.
- **Mind-bending concepts** — Ideas that are surreal or complex.

### Have you ever taken a course about stars?

I have never enrolled in a formal academic course on astronomy, which is something I somewhat regret. However, I frequently satisfy my curiosity by watching educational documentaries and reading articles online about astrophysics. I also love using stargazing apps on my smartphone to identify different constellations in the night sky. I would certainly love to take a structured class on the subject in the near future to deepen my understanding.

- **Enrolled in a formal course** — Officially signed up for a structured class.
- **Educational documentaries** — Non-fiction films designed to teach a topic.
- **Constellations** — Groups of stars forming recognizable patterns.
- **Deepen my understanding** — To learn much more thoroughly about a topic.

### Is it important to study stars?

Studying the stars is absolutely crucial because they are the fundamental building blocks of our universe. By analyzing stellar evolution, scientists can unlock mysteries about how time, matter, and galaxies were originally formed. Furthermore, tracking stars and celestial bodies has practical benefits, such as helping us navigate and predict cosmic hazards like solar flares. It truly puts our place in the universe into a clear perspective.

- **Stellar evolution** — The lifecycle and changing process of a star.
- **Fundamental building blocks** — Basic, essential pieces needed to make something.
- **Cosmic hazards** — Dangerous threats originating from space.
- **Clear perspective** — A sensible and realistic way of looking at things.

### Do you want to know more about outer space?

I possess an insatiable curiosity when it comes to learning about the mysteries of the cosmos. There is still so much that remains shrouded in mystery, such as dark matter and the existence of extraterrestrial life, which completely fascinates me. I try to stay updated with the latest discoveries made by space agencies like NASA through news articles and podcasts. It is a captivating subject that never fails to blow my mind.

- **Insatiable curiosity** — A desire to know things that cannot be satisfied.
- **Shrouded in mystery** — Completely hidden, secret, or unknown.
- **Extraterrestrial life** — Living organisms originating from outside Earth.
- **Captivating subject** — A highly interesting topic holding your full attention.

## 17. Headphones (May-Dec 2026)

### Do you use headphones?

Yes, I rely on headphones on a daily basis and consider them an indispensable accessory. I use them primarily to listen to music, catch up on podcasts, or take hands- free phone calls while on the move. They allow me to create my own personal sanctuary even when I am surrounded by a noisy crowd. I rarely leave my house without making sure they are packed in my bag.

- **Indispensable accessory** — An absolutely necessary item you cannot do without.
- **On the move** — Traveling or walking from place to place.
- **Personal sanctuary** — A private, safe, and peaceful place.
- **Packed in my bag** — Placed securely inside a backpack or handbag.

### In what situations would you use headphones?

Headphones are my go-to choice during my daily commute on public transit, as they help drown out the background noise. I also find them incredibly useful when I need to block out distractions and laser-focus on my tasks at work or study. Additionally, blasting an energetic playlist through my earbuds provides me with the perfect boost of motivation during intense gym workouts. They are perfect whenever I want an immersive audio experience.

- **Public transit** — Public transport systems like buses and trains.
- **Drown out** — Making a sound loud enough to block other noises.
- **Boost of motivation** — An increase in enthusiasm or energy.
- **Immersive audio experience** — Highly realistic sound that makes you feel surrounded.

### What type of headphones do you use?

I primarily use wireless, noise-canceling earbuds because they offer unmatched convenience and portability. The absence of tangled cords makes them incredibly hassle- free to use while walking or exercising. For long study sessions at home, however, I prefer over-ear headphones because they provide superior sound quality and better comfort for extended wear. It really depends on whether I prioritize mobility or audio fidelity at that moment.

- **Noise-canceling earbuds** — Small earphones that electronically block background sound.
- **Over-ear headphones** — Large headphones with padded cups covering the ears.
- **Extended wear** — Wearing or using something for a long time.
- **Audio fidelity** — How accurately and clearly sound is reproduced.

### In what situations would you NOT use headphones?

I strictly avoid wearing headphones while walking along busy streets or crossing roads because it poses a major safety hazard. It is vital to remain fully aware of my surroundings and hear oncoming traffic or pedestrian warning signals. I also think it is incredibly rude to keep them on during social gatherings or face-to-face conversations, as it signals a lack of engagement. In those moments, practicing situational awareness and courtesy is my top priority.

- **Safety hazard** — A dangerous situation that could cause an accident.
- **Social gatherings** — Events where people meet up to talk.
- **Engagement** — Showing active interest and involvement with others.
- **Situational awareness** — Understanding your environment to notice dangers.

## 18. Singing (May-Dec 2026)

Without a doubt, singing has an incredible capacity to lift people's spirits and bring immense joy. From a scientific standpoint, belted-out tunes release endorphins and oxytocin, which are known to alleviate stress and boost mood. It also serves as an amazing form of self-expression, allowing individuals to release bottled-up emotions in a healthy way. Whether it is singing solo in the shower or joining in a choir, it creates a powerful sense of emotional release.

- **Alleviate stress** — To reduce or ease feelings of tension.
- **Form of self-expression** — A way to show your unique thoughts or emotions.
- **Emotional release** — The healthy letting go of intense, hidden feelings.

## 19. Art (May-Dec 2026)

### Do you like modern art or traditional art?

Personally, I lean much more toward traditional art because I deeply admire the meticulous craftsmanship and realism found in classical oil paintings and sculptures. I feel that historic artworks often tell a very clear, captivating story that is easy to connect with. While I appreciate the avant-garde concepts behind modern art, I sometimes find it a bit too abstract and difficult to interpret. Traditional pieces simply resonate with my aesthetic preferences on a much deeper level.

- **Meticulous craftsmanship** — Extremely careful, detailed, skilled work by hand.
- **Classical oil paintings** — Traditional art pieces using pigment and drying oil.
- **Avant-garde concepts** — Ultra-modern, experimental, and unusual ideas.
- **Abstract** — Art that uses shapes and colors instead of realistic scenes.

### Do you like art?

Yes, I am a huge admirer of art in all its diverse forms, ranging from painting and photography to literature and architecture. For me, art is an essential medium through which human beings can express complex emotions, cultural values, and historical narratives. Visiting a museum or looking at a beautiful design never fails to spark my creativity and give me a fresh perspective on life. It truly adds immense color, depth, and meaning to our everyday mundane existence.

- **Essential medium** — A necessary tool or method used to express something.
- **Fresh perspective** — A new, clean way of looking at a situation.
- **Mundane existence** — A normal, boring, or unexciting everyday life.

### Have you ever visited an art gallery?

Yes, I have visited quite a few art galleries both locally and when traveling abroad to new cities. I distinctly remember wandering through a contemporary gallery a few months ago, which displayed a series of thought-provoking exhibitions. Standing directly in front of an original masterpiece allows me to appreciate the texture, scale, and intricate brushwork in a way that digital images can never replicate. It is always a deeply enriching and culturally stimulating experience for me.

- **Thought-provoking exhibitions** — Shows of art that inspire deep thinking.
- **Original masterpiece** — The actual, authentic piece of outstanding art.
- **Intricate brushwork** — Detailed, complex strokes made with a paintbrush.
- **Culturally stimulating** — Something that enriches your mind and interest in culture.

### Do you think it would be interesting for you to be an artist?

While I think the lifestyle of a creative professional sounds incredibly liberating, I do not believe I possess the natural talent or artistic flair required to succeed. Being able to translate abstract thoughts and complex emotions into a tangible visual format must be a highly fulfilling creative outlet. However, I suspect that dealing with the financial instability and constant pressure to produce original ideas could be quite nerve-wracking. I am perfectly content with simply being an appreciative observer rather than a creator.

- **Artistic flair** — A natural talent or style for creative activities.
- **Creative outlet** — An activity allowing you to express imagination.
- **Nerve-wracking** — Making you feel incredibly anxious or worried.

## 20. Clothes (May-Dec 2026)

### Do you usually wear T-shirts?

Yes, T-shirts are an absolute staple in my wardrobe, and I wear them almost every single day. They are my ultimate go-to option because they offer unbeatable comfort and are incredibly easy to style. Whether I am lounging at home, running quick errands, or meeting up with close friends, a simple tee is always appropriate. I love how they combine effortless practicality with a relaxed, casual aesthetic.

- **Absolute staple** — A basic, essential item always present in daily life.
- **Go-to option** — Your first and most reliable choice for a quick decision.
- **Running quick errands** — Going out to do short, necessary daily tasks.
- **Casual aesthetic** — A relaxed, informal, and comfortable style.

### Do you like wearing T-shirts?

I genuinely love wearing T-shirts, primarily because of the unmatched versatility they bring to daily fashion. They can be effortlessly dressed down with a pair of distressed jeans or dressed up under a sleek blazer for a smart-casual appearance. The soft, breathable fabric makes them perfect for staying comfortable throughout hot summer days. For me, they represent the ultimate balance between comfort and understated style.

- **Unmatched versatility** — Being incredibly adaptable for many situations.
- **Understated style** — An elegant fashion style that is subtle and not flashy.

### Do you like T-shirts with pictures and prints?

I generally prefer a minimalist style, so I tend to gravitate toward plain, solid-colored tees rather than those with bold graphic prints. However, I occasionally enjoy wearing shirts featuring subtle vintage designs or artistic illustrations that reflect my personality. I think graphic tees can serve as an excellent conversation starter and add a fun, playful element to an outfit. I just make sure the print is not too loud.

- **Bold graphic prints** — Large, bright, highly noticeable drawings on clothing.
- **Conversation starter** — An interesting item that easily starts a conversation.
- **Loud** — Flashy, bright, and designed to grab attention.

### What colour clothes do you like to wear?

My wardrobe consists mostly of neutral tones and earthy palettes, such as navy blue, charcoal gray, olive green, and classic black. I find these shades to be incredibly flattering, sophisticated, and easy to mix and match seamlessly. Occasionally, I might incorporate a vibrant pop of color, like a pastel pink or a deep crimson, to brighten up my look depending on my mood. Overall, muted colors dominate my daily fashion choices because they offer a timeless appeal.

- **Earthy palettes** — A group of colors inspired by nature (browns, greens, grays).
- **Timeless appeal** — Staying attractive and popular forever, despite changing trends.

### What are the differences between men and women's preference in colour?

Traditionally, societal norms have steered men toward darker, more subdued colors like blue, gray, and black, while women have embraced a wider spectrum, including pastels and vibrant shades. However, these rigid boundaries have blurred significantly in recent years, with modern fashion becoming much more gender-neutral. Today, color preferences are dictated far more by individual personality, unique style, and current runway trends rather than gender. Ultimately, fashion has become a tool for personal expression free from old-fashioned stereotypes.

- **Subdued colors** — Soft, quiet, or dark colors that do not grab attention.
- **Gender-neutral** — Suitable for or common to both men and women.
- **Runway trends** — Latest fashion ideas showcased by models at designer shows.

### What kind of clothes do you like to wear?

I am a huge advocate for smart-casual attire that seamlessly blends comfort with a polished appearance. My daily outfits usually involve well-fitted trousers, crisp button-down shirts, and comfortable loafers or clean sneakers. I always prioritize high-quality, breathable fabrics like cotton and linen because they feel amazing on the skin. Looking presentable while feeling completely at ease in my own clothes is my ultimate fashion philosophy.

- **Smart-casual attire** — Neat clothing blending professional and casual items.
- **Ultimate fashion philosophy** — A core, guiding belief rule regarding personal style.

### What kind of clothes do you never wear?

You would never catch me wearing overly formal, restrictive clothing like full three-piece suits or stiff tuxedos unless it is an absolute requirement for an event. I also completely steer clear of flashy garments covered in massive designer logos, as I find that style to be a bit too pretentious. Extremely tight or uncomfortable clothing is another absolute no-go for me. I firmly believe that fashion should never come at the expense of personal physical comfort.

- **Restrictive clothing** — Tight or stiff clothes limiting physical movement.
- **Pretentious** — Trying to appear more valuable or important to impress others.
- **Personal physical comfort** — The state of feeling physically at ease and free of pain.

### Do you wear the same style of clothes on weekdays and weekends?

There is actually a distinct contrast between my clothing choices for the workweek and the weekend. During weekdays, I adhere to a more structured, professional dress code to maintain a business-ready appearance for meetings and office tasks. On the weekends, however, I completely ditch the formal shirts and opt for ultra-casual loungewear, oversized hoodies, and sweatpants. This stylistic shift helps me mentally transition away from work and fully embrace a relaxed mindset.

- **Professional dress code** — Formal workplace rules dictating smart clothing.
- **Relaxed mindset** — A state of mind that is calm and stress-free.

## 21. Jokes (May-Dec 2026)

### Are you good at telling jokes?

To be perfectly honest, I am quite terrible at delivering jokes and rarely manage to get a laugh. I always seem to mess up the punchline or laugh prematurely before I even finish telling the story, which completely ruins the comedic effect. Instead of being a joker, I am much better suited to being an appreciative audience member who laughs at others. I definitely leave the humor to my friends who possess a natural comedic timing.

- **Punchline** — The final phrase or sentence of a joke providing the humor.
- **Comedic timing** — Knowing exactly when to say a line to maximize laughter.

### Do your friends like to tell jokes?

Yes, my friend circle is absolutely full of witty individuals who love to crack jokes and share hilarious anecdotes. There is one particular friend in our group who is practically a comedian and can turn even the most boring situation into something sidesplitting. Their sharp wit keeps our social gatherings lively, energetic, and incredibly entertaining. Having friends with a great sense of humor is something I deeply value.

- **Hilarious anecdotes** — Short, funny, entertaining real-life stories.
- **Sidesplitting** — Extremely funny, making you laugh until your stomach hurts.
