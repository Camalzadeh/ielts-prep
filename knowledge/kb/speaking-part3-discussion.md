# Speaking Part 3 Discussion Questions and Model Answers

Part 3 questions from the teacher's Topic 1–6 handouts, each linked to its Part 2 cue card (see [speaking-part2-cue-cards.md](speaking-part2-cue-cards.md)). Model answers are verbatim.

**Pattern of the model answers** (6–7 sentences, about 45–60 seconds spoken):
1. answer the question directly / give your overall view;
2. first reason with an explanation;
3. second reason or contrasting side (*On the other hand*, *However*, *In contrast*);
4. an example or a specific situation (*For instance*, *For example*, *my grandparents...*);
5. a closing sentence (*Ultimately*, *Therefore*, *Consequently*, *In short*).

Notice that the answers recycle the Part 2 vocabulary (e.g. *get away from it all*, *a hard pill to swallow*, *the early bird catches the worm*, *call a rain check*) — Part 3 is where those phrases earn Lexical Resource points. Part 3 is about people and society in general, so speak about *people*, *young people*, *my country* rather than only about yourself.


## Traveling (Part 2: *Place you'd like to visit in your free time*)

### Why do some people prefer to travel in their own country rather than going abroad?

For many people, domestic travel is the easiest way to relax and de-stress without any travel stress. Going to another country means dealing with passports, visas, and long flights, which can be very tiring. By staying in their own country, travelers can get away from it all with just a short drive or a quick train ride. This makes it much easier to clear one's mind and enjoy a peaceful break. Also, traveling locally eliminates language problems, so people can enjoy beautiful scenery while feeling safe and comfortable. Finally, local trips are usually cheaper because you do not have to buy expensive international plane tickets.

### Some people don't like to travel abroad. Why?

The main reason some people dislike international travel is the stress of being in an unfamiliar place. It can be hard to feel safe and enjoy a quiet atmosphere when you do not speak the local language. Many people also hate long journeys and do not want to spend their free time waiting in busy airports. On top of that, the cost of going overseas can become too expensive very quickly. Safety worries and confusing border rules can also scare people who just want to put their feet up and relax. In the end, they prefer familiar places where they can enjoy their holiday to their heart's content.

### Why do people choose to travel or live abroad?

Moving or traveling to another country is a wonderful way for people to learn new things and grow. Many people want to explore local history and look at breathtaking views that they cannot see at home. Living abroad also gives people a great chance to change their daily routine, practice a new language, and experience a lively lifestyle. From a work perspective, big international cities often have excellent job opportunities to build a good career. Others are simply following a long-held dream to see the world with their own eyes. Ultimately, living in a new country helps people become more independent and look at life in a fresh way.


## Tall building (Part 2: *Tall building*)

### What do you think buildings will be like in the future?

Future buildings will likely be eco-friendly designs that focus heavily on green technology. We will see more soaring skyscrapers covered in trees and plants to help clean the city air. These structures will use solar panels and rain collection systems, making them highly sustainable. Inside, state-of-the-art technology will automatically control the lights and temperature to save energy. I think future construction will also rely on smart materials that can repair their own cracks over time. Ultimately, these futuristic towers will act like small, self-contained cities where people can live, work, and grow food all in one place.

### Which do most people prefer, living in a bungalow or in a tall building?

Preferences usually depend on a person's age and lifestyle, but many families lean toward a bungalow. A single-story house offers a quiet, tranquil atmosphere and a private garden where children can play safely. In contrast, younger professionals often prefer high-rise apartments because they love the vibrant lifestyle of the city center. Living on a high floor provides breathtaking views and keeps you close to offices, shops, and restaurants. However, skyscrapers can feel crowded, and the lack of privacy is a hard pill to swallow for some. Ultimately, bungalows offer a sense of freedom, while tall buildings provide unmatched urban convenience.

### Why are taller and taller buildings being constructed nowadays?

The main reason for this trend is the rapid growth of the population in major metropolitan areas. Land downtown is extremely scarce and expensive, so developers must build upward to maximize limited space. Constructing a sprawling complex horizontally is no longer practical or affordable in big cities. These modern towers serve as multi-purpose facilities, packing offices, malls, and apartments into one vertical footprint. Furthermore, advanced engineering now allows cities to safely construct structures that literally reach for the sky. Finally, having a massive skyscraper acts as a powerful symbol of economic success and global status for a country.


## Getting up early (Part 2: *Getting up early*)

### Why do people get up early?

Many individuals choose to wake up at the crack of dawn to boost their daily productivity. Being an early riser allows people to utilize a highly productive window before the rest of the world wakes up and creates distractions. This quiet time offers a beautiful sense of solitude that is perfect for reading, studying, or exercising. Additionally, professionals often wake up bright and early to beat the morning rush hour traffic and get a head start on their work. Embracing this habit is also a fantastic way to enjoy a peaceful morning ritual, like drinking coffee without feeling rushed. Ultimately, they believe that the early bird catches the worm when it comes to achieving success.

### Are there any situations when it's not good to arrive early?

Yes, showing up ahead of schedule is not always ideal, especially when attending casual social gatherings like dinner parties. Arriving bright and early to a friend's house might accidentally put pressure on the host who is still preparing. It can disrupt their kitchen regimen or home setup, creating an awkward situation for everyone involved. Similarly, showing up too early for a job interview might make an applicant look overly anxious or desperate. In professional settings, arriving more than fifteen minutes early can disrupt the interviewer's tight schedule. Therefore, being too early can sometimes ruin the peaceful atmosphere or professional boundary you want to establish.

### Is it good to arrive early in any situation?

Generally speaking, arriving early is highly beneficial for formal, high- stakes appointments and travel schedules. For instance, reaching the airport or train station well ahead of time ensures you can handle unexpected queues without stress. It allows you to check in calmly, clear security, and avoid a last-minute alteration to your travel plans. Being early also shows strict discipline and respect for other people's time during professional business meetings. It gives you a moment to shake off the cobwebs, collect your thoughts, and feel rejuvenated before a presentation. Consequently, punctuality is almost always viewed as a positive trait that builds strong mutual trust.

### Why do some people stay up late at night?

A large number of individuals are naturally a night owl and find that their brains function best during late hours. For these people, the quietness of midnight provides a unique tranquility that helps them focus on creative projects or heavy studying. Others stay up late due to demanding work schedules or because they are burning the candle at both ends. Additionally, modern technology and entertainment streaming apps tempt people to look at screens long after they should be asleep. This digital habit makes it very easy to ignore bedtime and push back their natural sleep cycle. As a result, they trade their morning energy for a highly active, late-night routine.

### Is it easy to get up early?

For the vast majority of people, waking up early requires an immense amount of strict discipline and effort. It is incredibly tempting to hit the snooze button multiple times when the alarm goes off in the dark. If you do not get enough uninterrupted sleep, you will inevitably feel incredibly lethargic and sluggish in the morning. However, if you maintain a consistent sleeping regimen, your body clock will eventually adapt to the early hours. When you successfully form this habit, you actually wake up feeling completely rejuvenated and ready for the day. Therefore, while it is quite difficult at first, it becomes much easier with time and consistency.


## Growing a plant (Part 2: *Person who loves to grow plants*)

### Do people in your country like to grow plants?

Yes, gardening is an incredibly popular hobby among many generations in my country. A large number of households take great pride in their indoor and outdoor flora. Many elderly citizens view watering their flowers as a therapeutic morning ritual to start the day. Even in crowded urban areas, citizens try to cultivate small green spaces on their balconies. People love the feeling of transforming a plain concrete balcony into a nurturing environment filled with color. Ultimately, having a green thumb is highly respected, and local communities frequently share plant clippings with one another.

### What are the advantages of growing plants at home?

Bringing plant life into your living space offers a wide range of mental and physical benefits. Firstly, tending to indoor greenery provides a wonderful sense of tranquility after a stressful workday. Working with soil and pots gives city dwellers a peaceful moment of solitude away from digital screens. Furthermore, keeping house plants can drastically improve the air quality inside a modern apartment. It is also highly satisfying to watch a tiny seed finally germinate and grow into a healthy leaf. Therefore, domestic gardening is a fantastic, low-cost method to boost your overall well-being.

### Do people like to grow vegetables in your country?

Absolutely, cultivating edible crops is a traditional practice that remains very popular today. Many families own small countryside plots or allotments specifically dedicated to producing fresh food. They love to prepare the fertile soil every spring to ensure their crops grow strong. Even younger individuals are now trying to grow easy tomatoes or herbs in small pots. People prefer this because store-bought produce often lacks flavor and contains unwanted chemicals. Consequently, the desire for organic, homegrown food has made vegetable gardening a major trend.

### What are the advantages of growing vegetables at home?

The primary benefit of domestic food production is having instant access to organic, chemical-free nutrition. When you manage your own garden, you can ensure that the crops grow in completely fertile soil. It is also a highly economical habit that can save a family a lot of money on groceries. Managing a vegetable patch teaches children how nature works and encourages them to eat healthier foods. Additionally, learning how to care for a hardy species of vegetable builds great patience and life skills. In short, it is a rewarding practice that benefits both your health and your wallet.

### How do people feel when they eat vegetables that they grew on their own?

Eating a meal made from your own garden brings an immense sense of pride and personal satisfaction. After months of hard work, seeing your efforts finally bear fruit feels incredibly rewarding. The food always tastes significantly fresher and more delicious because of the time invested. Gathering a bountiful harvest from your backyard makes you feel self-sufficient and connected to the earth. It turns a simple, everyday dinner into a special celebration of your dedication and care. Ultimately, it is a joyful experience that cannot be replicated by simply buying food at a supermarket.


## Childhood friend (Part 2: *Childhood friend*)

### What's the difference between having younger friends and older friends?

Having younger friends usually brings a lot of high energy into your life because they love trying new trends. On the other hand, older friends have a lot of life experience and can give you great advice when you are confused. When I hang out with older people, we usually talk about deep life lessons and career goals. But when I am with younger friends, they keep me updated on popular music and modern culture. Ultimately, younger friends keep your spirit active, while older friends give you a strong sense of stability. It is definitely good to have both because they balance each other out perfectly. Therefore, a mix of young and old companions makes your social life much richer.

### Has technology changed people's friendships? How?

Yes, technology has completely changed how people connect with each other today. In the past, you had to live near someone to stay close and build a lifelong friendship. Nowadays, social media apps allow people to stay best friends even if they live in different countries. However, this big change has both good and bad sides. Even though we text all the time, these online chats can feel a bit empty and superficial. It is easy for busy people to drift apart if they never meet up in person to talk. Consequently, technology gives us more casual friends but makes it harder to build mutual trust.

### What do you think of communicating via social media?

Talking through social media is very convenient, but it lacks the warmth of real human contact. On the positive side, it is a fast way to share fun stories and daily updates with many people at once. It helps us keep in touch with old classmates when we are too busy to meet for coffee. However, text messages can easily be misunderstood, which sometimes causes unnecessary arguments. Relying only on likes and comments can make a close friendship feel distant over time. Therefore, social networks are great for quick updates but should not replace real conversations.

### Will online social media replace face-to-face communication?

I do not think online platforms will ever fully replace talking in person. Humans have a natural need to be physically close to the people they care about. While video calls are helpful, they cannot replace real eye contact, hugs, or body language. True friendships are built when people sit together, share food, and look each other in the eye. If we only talk through screens, people will start to feel very lonely and isolated. A smartphone screen can never act as a real shoulder to cry on when you are going through a hard time. Because of this, face-to-face talking will always be the most important way we connect.

### Is it necessary for kids to have some close friends?

Yes, having close playmates is incredibly important for a child's growth. These early friendships happen during their formative years when they are learning how the world works. By playing with close friends, children learn how to share, cooperate, and solve arguments nicely. These bonds teach them that good relationships require equal effort from both sides. Having a best friend also gives kids a safe space to talk about their school worries and fears. Without these early connections, children might struggle to trust others when they grow up. In short, childhood friends leave beautiful memories that shape a person's character forever.


## Plan to change (Part 2: *Plan you had to change*)

### Do old people often change plans?

Generally speaking, older generations do not like to alter their schedules and prefer to avoid making a spontaneous decision. They are usually very disciplined and appreciate routine, so they rarely change their minds at the eleventh hour. When they do make arrangements, they usually stick to them unless something serious happens. If a problem does pop up, they prefer to be pragmatic and fix it quietly rather than causing chaos. For instance, my grandparents always plan their days meticulously weeks in advance. Therefore, it is quite rare for seniors to leave their daily routines up in the air.

### Do young people like to change plans?

Yes, younger people are often much more flexible and actually enjoy making a spontaneous choice. They do not mind leaving their weekend schedules up in the air until the very last minute. If a better opportunity comes along, they are happy to experience a sudden change of heart and try something new. They are highly comfortable adjusting things on the fly because they can easily update their friends via smartphone apps. For them, life is fast-moving, so they prefer to play it by ear rather than sticking to rigid timetables. Consequently, altering arrangements is just a normal, everyday part of youth culture.

### What are the common reasons when people need to change plans?

In most cases, unexpected life events are the primary reason people have to reorganize their days. For example, a sudden schedule conflict at work or school can easily ruin your weekend arrangements. Bad weather can also force people to make a sudden detour if they are traveling or hosting an outdoor event. Health emergencies or catching a cold can also force an individual to call a rain check with their friends. Additionally, unexpected transport delays or flight cancellations happen all the time. When these situations arise, people must adjust quickly and rely on a contingency plan to save their day.

### How would you tell your friends if you had to change your plans?

If I absolutely had to make a last-minute alteration, I would call or text my friends immediately to explain the situation honestly. I believe it is best to be direct so they do not waste their time waiting around for me. I would apologize sincerely for the inconvenience, especially if the change happens at the eleventh hour. After explaining the reason, I would instantly suggest a new date to reschedule our meetup. For example, I might say, "I am so sorry, but I have a work emergency, so can we call a rain check for next Sunday?" Being polite and proactive helps prevent any hard feelings or misunderstandings.

### How does technology help people make plans?

Technology has made organizing our lives incredibly easy and highly efficient compared to the past. Shared digital calendars allow groups of friends to see each other's free time and avoid a schedule conflict. Messaging apps mean we can coordinate group events instantly without making dozens of separate phone calls. If a problem arises, we can improvise new arrangements in seconds via group chats. Navigation apps also help us avoid traffic by finding a quick sudden detour in real-time. In short, smartphones ensure that our schedules are never left haphazard or disorganized.

### Why do parents still make plans for their children nowadays?

Parents continue to manage their children's schedules because young kids lack the life experience to make pragmatic decisions. Left to themselves, children would live a very haphazard life focused only on playtime and sweets. Parents need to establish a structured routine during a child's formative years to ensure they balance school, hobbies, and rest. They manage the daily logistics because kids cannot handle a sudden schedule conflict on their own. Even though the world has changed, children still need their parents to act as a guide and an emotional anchor. Ultimately, this parental planning sets children up for a successful and organized adult life.
