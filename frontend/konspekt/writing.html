2. Overview: ən vacib abzas

Writing Task 1 - Structure.pdf · lövhə qeydləri

Task Achievement balı əsasən overview-dan asılıdır. Conclusion yazmırsan, onun işini overview görür.

Overall,+əsas trend (artım/azalma)+while / whereas+əks trend+In addition, max / min və ya liderin dəyişməsi

Overview-da olmalıdır

  • Overall, ilə başlayır
  • 1–2 cümlə
  • Ümumi trend: up / down / stable / fluctuating
  • Max / min: ən böyük, ən kiçik, ya da liderin dəyişməsi (surpassed it and ranked first)
  • while / whereas / In contrast ilə ziddiyyət

Overview-da olmamalıdır

  • Rəqəm, faiz, il. Əvəzinə: at the beginning / by the end of the period / in the final year
  • Kateqoriyaları sadəcə saymaq (trend və müqayisə olmadan)
  • Öz fikrin və ya qrafikdə olmayan səbəblər

Müəllimin overview nümunələri

Line graph: Australian exportsOverall, an upward trend was seen in the percentage of Australian goods exported to India and China, while the US and Japan witnessed an opposite trend over the period shown. In addition, at the beginning, Japan was the main importer of Australian goods, whereas in the last year China surpassed it and ranked first.
Line graph: residents of AustraliaOverall, whereas the proportion of citizens from the UK saw a downward trend, that of Asia and other regions experienced substantial upward trends over the mentioned period. Whilst the UK started at the highest point, it ended at the lowest one.

5. Zamanlar və proqnoz strukturları

lövhə qeydləri · Prediction Structures in IELTS Writing Task 1.pdf

Statik (bir il) qrafikdə trend sözləri yox, müqayisə dili lazımdır. Dinamik (illər üzrə) qrafikdə trend dili lazımdır. Zaman isə ön sözdən asılıdır:

Ön sözlərKeçmişGələcək
Simplein, from…to, between…and, during, over, at…rose to 90 in Germany in 2000.…will rise to 90 in Germany in 2030.
Perfectby, until…had risen to 90 in Germany by 2000.…will have risen to 90 in Germany by 2030.
by + year → had + V3!

Proqnoz (2030, 2040 kimi illər, planlaşdırılan xəritələr)

#StrukturNümunə
1Subject + will + verb + … + timeThe GDP will rise dramatically up to $415 billion in 2023.
2According to current + plural noun, there will be …According to current predictions, there will be 1000 new cases in this city within 2 months.
3It is + V3 + that + subject + will …It is predicted that the eastern mainland of Ho Chi Minh City will be completely submerged by 2050.
4Subject + is/are + V3 + to + verbBy 2023, Vietnam is expected to reach its export target.
5Subject + is/are + likely + to + verbAn aging population is likely to lead to a shortage of workers in technology sectors in 2030.

Hər kateqoriya üçün fərqli struktur işlət, hər cümlədə will yazma. Variantlar: is projected / forecast / set to.

Model: Australian exports to four countriesmüəllimin modeli
The line graph shows the percentages of Australian exports to four countries. Summarize the information by selecting and reporting the main features and make comparisons where relevant.

IntroThe line graph illustrates the proportion of Australian exports to four distinct countries, namely Japan, the United States, China, and India, over the period from 1990 to 2012.

OverviewOverall, an upward trend was seen in the percentage of Australian goods exported to India and China, while the US and Japan witnessed an opposite trend over the period shown. In addition, at the beginning, Japan was the main importer of Australian goods, whereas in the last year China surpassed it and ranked first.

Body 1Exports to Japan were the highest at the beginning, standing at approximately 26 billion in 1990. However, this figure decreased consistently over the period, reaching around 17 billion in 2012. Meanwhile, exports to the US fluctuated. They started at about 11 billion, fell to 8 billion in 1995, then recovered to roughly the same level in 2000 before declining slightly again towards the end of the period.

Body 2By comparison, exports to China began at a very low level of around 1 billion in 1990 but rose sharply throughout the period. By 2012, they had surged to approximately 27 billion, overtaking Japan and the US to become Australia's largest export market. Similarly, exports to India increased steadily from about 2 billion to around 7 billion in 2010, before experiencing a minor decline in the final years.

Niyə işləyir
  • Overview iki cümlədir: trendlər (an upward trend… while… an opposite trend) və liderin dəyişməsi. Bir dənə də rəqəm yoxdur.
  • Body 1 artmayan xətlərdir (Japan, US), Body 2 artanlar (China, India).
  • By 2012, they had surged: by + il → past perfect.

Line graph üçün hazır qəliblər

  • X began at a very low level of around … in 1990 but rose sharply throughout the period.
  • X fell consistently over the following decades, dropping to 10% in 1986 and then to just 5% by 2011, making it the smallest contributor at the end of the period.
  • …reaching a peak of around 14% in 2006 before ending slightly lower at about 13% in 2011.
  • Total numbers remained between 19 and 25 million throughout the period.

The bar chart below shows numbers of seals, whales and dolphins recorded in the Gormez Straits from 2006 to 2018.

IntroThe bar chart illustrates the populations of seals, whales, and dolphins documented in the Strait of Gormez over a twelve-year period from 2006 to 2018.

OverviewOverall, all three marine species experienced population growth over the period, with dolphins witnessing the most dramatic increase. In addition, as a result of a sharp rise, the number of dolphins surpassed that of seals to become the highest one in the final year.

Body 1In 2006, the seal population stood at approximately 27, before rising significantly to around 40 by 2010. However, this figure saw a decline to about 35 in 2014 before climbing again to nearly 50 by 2018. The whale population exhibited a steady upward trend, starting at 15 in 2006 and reaching 30 by 2018, after rising moderately to roughly 20 in 2010 and 25 in 2014.

Body 2In contrast to seals and whales, the dolphin population experienced substantial growth throughout the period. Starting at a modest 10 in 2006, the number of dolphins rose to around 15 in 2010. This growth continued dramatically, culminating in 55 dolphins recorded in 2018. Notably, the dolphin population surpassed both seals and whales by 2018, highlighting a significant shift in marine life in the Strait of Gormez.

Model 2 (statik): doctors' and other workers' pay, 2004müəllimin modeli
The chart below shows the annual pay (thousands of US dollars) for doctors and other workers in seven countries in 2004.

IntroThe bar chart illustrates how the occupational earnings for doctors and other workers varied across seven nations in 2004.

OverviewOverall, physicians consistently commanded higher salaries than other professionals in all countries surveyed. The United States topped the list for both doctors and other workers in terms of annual income, while income in Italy, the Czech Republic, Germany and Finland was relatively similar and low for both categories.

Body 1Doctors' annual salaries exhibited a wide range across the seven countries, from approximately $50,000 to $120,000. The United States led with a substantial advantage, paying doctors an average of $120,000 per year, significantly more than the joint second-place holders, France and Switzerland, at around $70,000. In contrast, Finland recorded the lowest income for doctors at $50,000, which was $10,000 less than Italy.

Body 2Regarding other workers, the United States also held the top spot with an average annual income of just over $40,000. Meanwhile, other professionals in France and Finland earned slightly less, making roughly $30,000 and $25,000 respectively. Notably, the remaining three countries paid their workers roughly $20,000 annually.

Niyə işləyir
  • Bir ildir, ona görə trend feli yoxdur. Hər şey sıralama və müqayisədir.
  • Abzaslar qrupa görə bölünüb (doctors, sonra other workers), hər birində ən yüksəkdən ən aşağıya.
  • Vahidə bax: oxda “thousands of US dollars” yazılıb, deməli 120 = $120,000.

Model: transport to a university, 2004 vs 2009müəllimin modeli
The charts show the main methods of transportation for people travelling to a university for work or study in 2004 and 2009.

IntroThe pie charts illustrate the percentage of students and staff utilizing different modes of transportation to travel to a university in 2004 and 2009.

OverviewOverall, private cars were the most popular means of transport at the beginning of the period, while they were overtaken by buses as the primary choice in the final year. The percentage of people traveling by bus, train, walking and bicycle increased, whereas the figure for car decreased over the five-year period.

Body 1Cars accounted for over half of all university commutes in 2004, at 51%. However, this figure decreased considerably to 28% in 2009. Although the proportion of commuters taking the bus stood lower than that of cars in 2004, at 33%, it experienced a sharp increase to reach 46% by 2009, making it the top transit method.

Body 2Regarding the remaining categories, the share of people traveling by bicycle saw a significant climb, rising from 9% to 16% between 2004 and 2009. Walking to the campus accounted for just 4% of journeys in 2004 before experiencing a slight rise to 6% in 2009. Finally, train travel was the least popular option, utilized by a mere 3% of travelers in 2004, after which it grew marginally to 4% by the end of the period.

Niyə işləyir
  • Body 1 yerini dəyişən iki böyük seqmentdir (car, bus). Body 2 qalan üç kiçik seqmentdir.
  • Hər cümlədə hər iki il var: rising from 9% to 16% between 2004 and 2009.

Bir pie üçün (Brighton graduates): service industries held the largest market share by a wide margin… constituted exactly one-third of the total (33%). This was followed by… charity (0.3%) and sport (0.1%) both attracted less than 1% of applicants.

IntroThe table shows the percentage of women in senior management positions in three companies from 1975 to 2015.

IntroThe line graph shows the number of visits to and from the United Kingdom between 1979 and 1999, while the bar chart shows the five most popular holiday countries for UK residents in 1999.

OverviewOverall, both the number of UK residents traveling abroad and foreign visitors coming to the UK increased over the 20-year period. However, UK residents traveling abroad always outnumbered foreign visitors. France and Spain were the most popular choices for UK tourists.

Model: big salary vs job satisfaction (disagree)müəllimin modeli
A big salary is much more important than job satisfaction. Do you agree or disagree?

In today's world, many people choose to focus on earning a high salary instead of finding a job they truly love. While having enough money is definitely important, the value of enjoying your work should not be ignored. I disagree with the idea that a big paycheck is more important than job satisfaction, because long-term happiness and health depend heavily on how happy you are with your daily work.

To begin with, job satisfaction plays a massive role in a person's mental health and everyday happiness. When people spend more than forty hours a week at a job, the emotional environment of the workplace changes their entire life. Doing work that matters makes people feel successful, proud, and emotionally stable, which naturally protects them from anxiety and depression. On the other hand, a high-stress job that feels meaningless quickly destroys a person's mood, regardless of how much money they make, because human happiness relies on purpose rather than just a large bank account. A great example of this is seen in charity organizations like Doctors Without Borders, where doctors willingly give up the huge salaries of private hospitals to help people in need, leading to deep personal happiness despite working in dangerous areas.

Furthermore, workers who love their jobs are generally much more creative, productive, and loyal to their companies. When an employee feels genuinely excited about their duties, they do not just do the bare minimum; instead, they invest their personal energy into finding better ways to solve problems. This high level of commitment makes them stand out to their managers, which naturally leads to fast career growth, promotions, and eventually, even higher pay over time. Therefore, passion does not mean staying poor; it is actually a reliable path to true financial success because excellent performance is driven by genuine interest. This situation is clearly shown by business research from companies like Gallup, which consistently proves that happy and connected workers make their teams 21% more profitable than teams that are unhappy and disconnected.

In conclusion, while a big salary offers clear material benefits, I strongly believe that loving your career is much more important for long-term happiness and health.

Cəmiyyətdə bir trend var, onu qiymətləndirirsən. Müəllim birtərəfli cavab tövsiyə edir: “completely positive” və ya “completely negative”, iki səbəblə. Hər body abzası result cümləsi ilə bitir.

Model: working from home (positive)300 söz
More and more people are choosing to work from home. Is this a positive or negative development?

The growing trend of remote work represents a major shift in how modern society operates today. I completely agree that this change is an entirely positive development because it improves daily time management and enhances employee's mental health.

First, eliminating the daily journey to an office allows individuals to utilize their time much more efficiently. Traditional office employment forces workers to spend hours traveling every week, which leads to physical exhaustion and unnecessary transportation costs. In contrast, remote employees can easily redirect these lost hours toward healthy habits like preparing nutritious food, exercising daily, or getting sufficient sleep. For instance, data from the National Bureau of Economic Research confirms that remote workers save over one hour per day on average. This extra time is directly used for family care, domestic duties, and personal health, which makes daily life much more manageable and less rushed for the average professional.

Second, the structural flexibility of a home workspace directly supports better mental health for the contemporary workforce. Conventional office spaces are often filled with stressful distractions, loud noises, and strict schedules that increase employee anxiety. Working from home allows individuals to control their immediate environment, create a quiet atmosphere, and take brief breaks to recover their focus without constant observation. This sense of personal autonomy reduces professional burnout and increases overall job satisfaction. A clear example of this is seen in modern technology firms, where employees who choose their own hours report much lower stress levels, fewer sick days, and higher morale than those in rigid office settings.

In conclusion, the transition toward remote employment is a thoroughly beneficial change for the global workforce. By saving valuable time on commuting and providing a less stressful environment, this practice ensures that workers enjoy better health and a superior balance between life and work.

Model: cars vs bicycles in a citymüəllimin modeli

It is often argued that private vehicles offer the most efficient means of urban transportation, whereas others contend that bicycles represent a superior alternative for city travel. While automobiles provide unmatched personal comfort, I believe that bicycles are the optimal mode of transport within urban environments due to their efficiency in traffic and environmental sustainability.

On the one hand, many people maintain that commuting by car is the most advantageous method of navigating a city. Proponents of this view emphasize the convenience, safety, and physical comfort that personal vehicles provide, especially during inclement weather or when traveling long distances. Furthermore, cars allow passengers to transport heavy goods or travel with family members seamlessly. A clear example of this can be seen in sprawling modern cities like Dubai or Houston, where expansive highway systems are designed specifically for automotive travel, making cars practically indispensable for daily commuting and commercial activities.

Additionally, utilizing bicycles promotes public health through cardiovascular exercise and significantly reduces urban air pollution. For instance, the municipal government of Amsterdam heavily invested in dedicated cycling infrastructure, resulting in a system where bicycles now outnumber cars, drastically reducing inner-city travel times and local carbon emissions.

In conclusion, although private cars offer superior comfort and utility for long-distance travel, they frequently contribute to urban congestion and environmental degradation. Ultimately, I maintain that bicycles are the superior choice for city travel because they facilitate faster transit through congested areas while fostering healthier and more sustainable urban communities.

Model: e-books vs paper books312 söz
With the rise of e-books comes the decline in paper books. Some people see this as a good step forward while others do not. What are the advantages and disadvantages of this?

Nowadays, the increasing popularity of e-books has led to a decline in the use of traditional paper books. While this shift offers clear benefits regarding convenience and accessibility, it also introduces notable drawbacks concerning reading habits and technological dependence, making it a development with equally balanced positive and negative impacts.

One of the most significant advantages of e-books is cost-efficiency. Since electronic books do not require printing, binding, or physical shipping, both publishers and consumers save a substantial amount of money. As a result, readers can buy a wide variety of titles at a much lower price than printed versions. Another key benefit is the flexibility that digital readers offer, as individuals can store hundreds of books on a single device and carry them easily while traveling. This convenience is especially helpful for students or professionals who need to access multiple textbooks at the same time. For example, a university student can now carry an entire semester's worth of heavy reading materials inside a single lightweight tablet.

However, the widespread adoption of e-books is not without drawbacks. Many readers argue that digital screens cannot replace the physical pleasure of holding a real book and turning its actual pages. For avid readers, this traditional sensory experience is an essential part of literacy that technology simply cannot replicate. In addition, electronic reading is completely dependent on digital devices, meaning that users are vulnerable to battery loss and unexpected technical glitches. A device can easily run out of power or malfunction, making it unreliable during emergencies. A clear illustration of this occurs when a traveler is left unable to read their novel during a long flight simply because they forgot to bring a charging cable.

In conclusion, e-books provide undeniable cost savings and unmatched portability; however, these benefits are countered by the loss of the physical reading experience and a vulnerable dependence on battery life.

Model: children watching TV (disadvantages outweigh)317 söz
Today children spend a lot of their free time watching TV. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages of this practice?

It is common for contemporary children to devote a significant portion of their leisure time to watching television. I firmly believe that the drawbacks of this practice, specifically regarding physical health and social development, far outweigh the single advantage of information.

On the one hand, excessive television viewing severely damages the physical well-being of young individuals. Sitting in front of a screen for extended periods promotes an inactive lifestyle, which eliminates physical movement and alters metabolism to cause severe, lifelong medical conditions. For instance, the World Health Organization reported that children who watch more than three hours of television daily suffer from a forty percent higher rate of childhood diabetes and chronic spinal posture deformities. Furthermore, this passive habit acts as a major barrier to healthy childhood socialization. When children prioritize screens over real-life interactions, they fail to practice vital interpersonal skills such as sharing, conflict resolution, and empathy. For example, a recent study by Harvard University tracked primary school pupils and found that those with heavy television habits were twice as likely to exhibit social anxiety and struggle to form meaningful friendships with classmates.

On the other hand, television can serve as an accessible tool for global education and information. However, this benefit is weak because television remains a completely passive learning medium that lacks interactive engagement, and children often consume uninformative entertainment rather than educational content. For instance, global media statistics show that eighty percent of children under the age of twelve choose to watch fictional, fast-paced cartoons or commercial advertisements rather than educational nature programs like National Geographic, which ultimately nullifies the intellectual value of the medium.

In conclusion, while television is a rich source of information, it should not replace active pastimes. The severe risks it poses to a child's physical health and interpersonal skills mean that the disadvantages clearly outweigh the advantages. Parents must therefore regulate screen time to ensure balanced development.

Model: overpopulation of urban areas350 söz
Overpopulation of urban areas has led to numerous problems. Discuss the problems and suggest ways that the government and individuals can tackle these problems.

Overpopulation in cities has become a serious issue, putting a heavy strain on government resources and public systems. While this trend causes major problems with severe air and water pollution, the most effective solution is for governments to move people out of crowded cities by developing rural areas.

The main result of overpopulation is the huge rise in pollution across city areas. As the number of people grows, the amount of daily trash and car exhaust quickly becomes too much for city systems to handle. The most immediate danger comes from car smoke, as millions of residents use cars and motorbikes to get to work, which fills the air with dirty gas and thick smog. Additionally, crowded cities struggle to manage household and factory waste. When local systems cannot keep up with the massive amount of garbage made every day, dirty waste and sewage often end up in local rivers, ruining clean water sources and killing local wildlife. A clear example of this is New Delhi, where overcrowding has caused dangerous winter smog and severe river pollution.

However, the best way to solve these pollution problems is for governments to lower the number of people living in big cities. Governments can do this by spending money to build up rural areas and smaller towns, making sure they have good schools, modern hospitals, and fast internet. Furthermore, the government can offer lower taxes and cheaper land to companies that choose to open their offices or factories outside of big cities. When good jobs and great public services are available in the countryside, many citizens will naturally choose to move away from crowded zones, which quickly cuts down city pollution. A prime example of this strategy is South Korea, which successfully moved thousands of workers out of crowded Seoul by building a completely new city called Sejong.

In conclusion, although overpopulation creates difficult challenges like severe air and water pollution, smart government actions can solve these issues. By improving smaller towns and helping people move out of crowded places, governments can clean up city environments and make life better across the country.

Çox həll variantı (plastik tullantı modeli): First, governments should launch national campaigns… Second, teamwork between authorities and private businesses can… Finally, states must pass stricter laws… A prime example of this joint effort is seen in the European Union, where member states successfully banned single-use plastics.

Cause / effect

Cause_Effect Essay - Structure.pdf

Bütün səbəblər Body 1-də, bütün nəticələr Body 2-də. Thesis artıq səbəbi və nəticəni adlandırır. Sual “on people and society” deyirsə, nəticə abzasında For individuals, … For society, … yaz.

Model: overweight people325 söz
Today more people are overweight than ever before. What are the primary causes of this? What are the main effects of this epidemic on people and society?

In many countries around the world, the number of people who are overweight is increasing rapidly. This trend is mainly driven by modern office-based jobs that limit physical movement, and it results in severe health problems for individuals as well as heavy financial burdens for society.

The primary cause of rising obesity rates is the inactive nature of modern work cultures. Today, the demands of the contemporary workplace force millions of people to sit at desks in front of computers for most of the day. This lack of daily movement means that people burn far fewer calories than they consume, which directly leads to steady weight gain over time. Furthermore, long working hours leave individuals with very little free time or energy to exercise after their shifts finish. For example, a study by Harvard University found that the average global workweek has increased by 10 hours over the last 40 years, which matches the exact period where global obesity rates tripled.

The effects of this widespread weight gain are deeply damaging to both individuals and the wider community. For individuals, carrying excess weight is directly linked to dangerous, long-term illnesses like heart disease, strokes, and diabetes, which drastically reduce a person's lifespan. For society, these health issues create a massive crisis for public healthcare systems, which must spend billions treating preventable conditions. Additionally, obesity harms the economy because sick employees take more days off work and are less productive when they are at their jobs. To illustrate, the American Diabetes Association reported that diabetes and related weight complications cost the United States $327 billion in a single year, with $90 billion of that total caused entirely by lost workplace productivity and early deaths.

In conclusion, desk-bound office jobs and a lack of daily exercise are the main reasons why obesity is skyrocketing. This problem not only ruins the physical health of individuals but also places a massive financial strain on medical systems and global economies.

Model: professionals leaving poorer countries315 söz
An increasing number of professionals, such as doctors and teachers, are leaving their own poorer countries to work in developed countries. What are the causes of this problem? What can be done to deal with this situation?

An increasing number of skilled professionals, such as school teachers and medical doctors, are leaving developing nations to work in richer countries. This essay will examine how unemployment and low salaries cause this problem, and explain why government action is the most realistic solution to this global issue.

The primary reason why these experts choose to migrate is the high level of unemployment in their home countries. When developing nations face economic problems, there are not enough jobs created for university graduates. This means that even qualified individuals cannot find work, leaving them with no choice but to look for jobs abroad. The second major cause is the low salary given to those who find work. In poorer nations, wages are so low that workers cannot afford the basic cost of living. As a result of these low salaries, they feel unhappy and are forced to leave their homes to find a better life elsewhere. For instance, reports show thousands of teachers leave Asia annually due to long periods of unemployment and poor pay.

The primary solution is for governments to create more jobs. When states invest in building schools and hospitals, they open up many positions for graduates. This allows educated people to use their skills at home instead of moving away. The second step is for employers to increase the basic salary. When local wages are raised, professionals can easily cover their daily living costs. As a consequence of these higher salaries, they feel valued and choose to stay in their homes to build a better future locally. For example, the Philippines recently introduced laws to upgrade medical salaries. Eventually, health worker migration dropped significantly, keeping talent inside the country.

In conclusion, a combination of high unemployment and poor wages causes professionals to leave. However, this issue can be solved if governments improve working conditions and give experts a strong reason to stay.

Model: fewer people write by handmüəllimin modeli
Fewer and fewer people today write by hand using a pen or pencil. What are the reasons for this? Is this a positive or a negative development?

In the contemporary digital era, the traditional practice of handwriting with a pen or pencil is rapidly declining. This shift is primarily driven by the ubiquity of digital devices and the demand for workplace efficiency, and I consider this to be a highly positive development because it enhances global communication speed and reduces paper consumption.

The primary reason for the decline in manual writing is the rapid advancement and widespread accessibility of digital technology. In both educational and corporate environments, electronic devices such as laptops, smartphones, and tablets have replaced physical stationery. Typing on a keyboard or touchscreen is fundamentally faster and more efficient than writing by hand, allowing individuals to process and document information at an unprecedented pace. For example, in modern universities worldwide, students routinely utilize cloud-based note-taking applications like Notion or Microsoft OneNote during lectures. This allows them to synchronize their notes across multiple devices instantly and search through vast amounts of text in seconds, a feat that is entirely impossible with traditional notebooks.

I firmly believe that this transition toward digital communication is a profoundly positive development. Firstly, the widespread adoption of typing significantly boosts productivity and streamlines professional workflows across global industries. Digital text can be instantly edited, formatted, and shared across the world, eliminating geographical barriers to collaboration. Secondly, moving away from paper-based documentation offers immense environmental benefits. For instance, large multinational corporations like Google and Amazon have implemented strict "paperless office" initiatives, shifting all internal documentation, contracts, and invoicing to secured digital clouds. This structural change saves millions of trees annually, drastically lowering the corporate carbon footprint and reducing industrial waste.

In conclusion, the shift away from handwriting is a direct consequence of technological integration and the need for operational speed. Ultimately, this change is highly beneficial because it optimizes human productivity while actively contributing to global environmental conservation.

Some people believe that drivers should take a driving test every year. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
Opinion. Bir tərəf + 2 səbəb, hər ikisi sənin mövqeyini dəstəkləyir.
Some people believe that it is possible for a country to be economically successful and have a clean environment. Others argue that these two objectives are not compatible. Discuss both the views and give your opinion.
Discussion. Body 1: razı olmadığın fikir, Body 2: razı olduğun. Fikir girişdə və sonda.
As countries develop, more fast-food outlets move into the country. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?
Outweigh. Güclü tərəfə 2 nöqtə, zəif tərəfə 1 nöqtə və this benefit is weak because…
In many countries today, people in cities either live alone or in small family units, rather than in large, extended family groups. Is this a positive or negative trend?
Positive / negative. Tam müsbət və ya tam mənfi, 2 səbəb, hər abzas result cümləsi ilə bitir.
Each year, the crime rate increases. What are the causes of crime and what could be done to prevent this rise in criminal activity?
Cause / solution. Müəllimin şablonu; həllər səbəblərə uyğun gəlsin. Misal: Norway's rehabilitation-focused prisons.
An increasing number of people are choosing to have cosmetic surgery in order to improve their appearance. Why do people have operations to change the way they look? Do you think this is a positive or negative development?
Direct question. Body 1: səbəblər (why), Body 2: müsbət/mənfi. Outline statement hər ikisinə cavab verir.
News editors decide what to broadcast on television and what to print in newspapers. What factors do you think influence these decisions? Would it be better if more good news was reported?
Direct question. Body 1: faktorlar, Body 2: yaxşı xəbər daha çox olmalıdırmı (fikrin).
People often argue that health care services should not be used for making profit. Do the disadvantages of private healthcare outweigh its advantages?
Outweigh. Diqqət: sual “disadvantages outweigh advantages” soruşur. Thesis-də birbaşa cavab ver.
Money should be spent on creating new public buildings such as museums or town halls rather than renovating the existing ones. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
Opinion. I completely / partly agree; iki səbəb.
